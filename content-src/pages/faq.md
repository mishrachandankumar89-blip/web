## AI search and GEO {wide}

^ Section I

::: faq
? What is Generative Engine Optimization?
Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, is the work of making your brand easy for AI assistants to find, trust and quote. It covers crawlable pages, consistent business facts, answer-first content with evidence, and mentions on trusted sites. Read our [GEO service page](service:generative-engine-optimization) for the full method.

? Where does the term GEO come from?
It comes from a research paper titled "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization" by researchers from Princeton, Georgia Tech, the Allen Institute for AI and IIT Delhi, presented at the KDD 2024 conference. It found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content more visible in generative answers.

? Does Google have special rules for AI Overviews?
Google says there are no additional requirements or special optimizations for AI Overviews or AI Mode. A page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet, and the usual guidance on helpful, reliable content applies.

? Can we see what AI assistants say about our brand?
Yes. We run a fixed set of questions about your category and brand through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google, record the answers and sources, and track how they change over time.

? Should we let AI crawlers access our website?
For most businesses that want to be recommended, yes. Search and answer crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot need access to cite you. Training crawlers are a separate decision you can make per crawler in robots.txt.

? What is llms.txt?
A proposed standard file, placed at the root of a website, that gives AI tools a short, structured summary of the site and links to its key pages. It is cheap to add, and this website generates one automatically. It is not a ranking factor, and Google's May 2026 guide to generative AI search says llms.txt gets no special treatment in Search.
:::

## SEO and content {wide alt}

^ Section II

::: faq
? How long does SEO take?
Google's own guidance says SEO improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Technical fixes on an established site can work faster; new sites in competitive markets take longer.

? Is SEO still worth doing with AI answers on the rise?
Yes. AI Overviews, AI Mode and most AI assistants draw on pages that rank and are indexed. SEO decides whether you are in the pool of sources at all.

? Is AI-written content bad for SEO?
Google says it rewards helpful content however it is produced, but it also treats producing many pages mainly to manipulate rankings as scaled content abuse. We use AI as a research aid and write, edit and fact-check every piece ourselves.

? How many blog posts should we publish?
Fewer, better pieces win. Two to four researched articles a month, plus updates to existing pages, usually beats a high volume of thin posts.

? Do backlinks still matter?
Yes. Relevant links and mentions from trusted sites remain a strong signal for search engines and give AI assistants independent confirmation of who you are. Bought links that break Google's policies can do serious harm.
:::

## Ads, social and websites {wide}

^ Section III

::: faq
? Should we run Google Ads or invest in SEO?
Often both. Ads bring enquiries quickly and show which searches convert. SEO builds traffic you do not pay for per click. As organic rankings grow, ad budgets can shift to new opportunities.

? Which social platform is best for our business?
The one your buyers use while they are in a buying mindset. LinkedIn suits most B2B services, Instagram and Facebook suit consumer and local businesses, and YouTube works for anything that needs explaining.

? How fast should our website be?
Google's Core Web Vitals call Largest Contentful Paint good at 2.5 seconds or less, Interaction to Next Paint good at 200 milliseconds or less, and Cumulative Layout Shift good at 0.1 or less, measured on real visits.

? Will a redesign hurt our rankings?
It can if URLs change without redirects or content is removed. With a proper migration plan, a redesign should protect rankings and often improves them.
:::
