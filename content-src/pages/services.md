## How the services work together

Search engines and AI assistants judge your business on everything they can find. That is why we plan services as one system.

### AI visibility: GEO and AEO

Built on top of strong SEO. GEO earns citations inside AI-written answers; AEO wins featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice replies. Both depend on clear, evidence-backed pages and consistent facts about your brand.

### Search: SEO, technical, local and e-commerce

The foundation. Technical SEO makes pages readable, SEO makes them relevant and competitive, local SEO wins the map, and e-commerce SEO handles the special problems of large catalogues.

### Authority: content and digital PR

Content gives people and machines something worth reading and quoting. Digital PR gets that content, your data and your experts noticed by the sites that shape opinion.

### Demand and conversion: ads, social, web and CRO

Google Ads captures demand that exists today. Social media creates demand for tomorrow. Your website and conversion work make sure every visit has the best chance of becoming a customer.

## Questions about our services {wide}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Do we have to buy several services?
No. Starting with one service is common and often sensible. We recommend combinations only when one service clearly depends on another, for example GEO on a site with serious technical problems.

? How is pricing worked out?
Each engagement is scoped and quoted after a discovery call, based on your goals, market, site size and how much work your team will handle. We do not sell fixed packages that ignore those differences.

? Is there a minimum contract?
Ongoing services such as SEO, GEO and paid media need a few months to show their value, so we usually plan in quarters. Projects such as audits and website builds have a defined scope and end date.

? Can you work with our existing agency?
Yes. We can handle one area, such as AI visibility or technical SEO, while another agency or your in-house team handles the rest.
:::
