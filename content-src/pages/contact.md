### Speak to us directly

[[contact_details]]

The quickest route is a WhatsApp message with your website address and one line about your goal. We will reply with a few questions and a time for a call.

### What to have ready

- Your website address and any other sites you run
- Your main goal for the next six to twelve months
- Your top two or three competitors
- Any current agency work or recent changes to the site

## What happens after you get in touch {wide alt}

^ Next steps

::: steps
- **We reply.** Within one working day, with a few questions and suggested times for a call.
- **Strategy call.** Thirty to forty-five minutes on your goals, market, current marketing and constraints.
- **Quick review.** We look at your site, your rankings and how AI assistants describe you before we make any recommendation.
- **Proposal.** A clear scope, timeline and quote, focused on the first ninety days.
- **Kick-off.** Access, baseline measurement and the first priorities in motion.
:::

## Questions before you contact us {wide}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is the first call free?
Yes. The strategy call is there to understand your situation and decide whether we are the right fit. There is no obligation to continue.

? Do you sign NDAs?
Yes. If your project involves sensitive information, we are happy to sign a reasonable non-disclosure agreement before you share it.

? Which languages do you work in?
We work in English and Hindi, and we can plan content in other Indian languages where your buyers search in them.

? Can we meet in person?
Most of our work happens online, which keeps it fast and affordable. For larger projects we can arrange in-person meetings when they add value.
