## Why AVENZA exists

> For twenty years, online visibility meant one thing: rank on page one. That definition is no longer enough.

Today a buyer might ask ChatGPT to shortlist three accountants, check what Google's AI Overview says about a treatment, watch two short videos on Instagram and only then visit a website. Each of those moments is a chance to be named, or to be skipped.

Most marketing is still organised in silos. One team chases rankings, another runs ads, a third posts on social, and nobody asks what an AI assistant says about the brand. The result is a lot of activity and very little clarity.

We started AVENZA to join those pieces into one strategy, built around a simple question: **when your buyer asks, are you the answer?**

## What we believe

::: grid
- **Evidence beats volume.** One page with original data and real experience outperforms ten pages that repeat the internet.
- **Foundations come first.** No AI tactic can save a site that crawlers cannot read or buyers cannot trust.
- **Honesty is a strategy.** We tell clients what we do not know, what will take time and what we cannot promise.
- **Clients own their growth.** Accounts, data, content and tracking always belong to the client.
:::

## How we are different

The tools and channels we use are the same ones every serious agency uses. What differs is how we decide what to do and how we hold ourselves to account.

| Common practice | How AVENZA works |
|---|---|
| Reports full of rankings and traffic | Reports tied to enquiries, revenue and AI citations |
| A separate plan for every channel | One visibility strategy across search, AI, ads, social and site |
| Content produced in bulk | Fewer, deeper pieces with expert input and cited sources |
| Link packages sold by the hundred | Earned coverage and relevant links only |
| Advice from habit | Advice from primary sources, tests and your own data |

## Who we work with

We do our best work with businesses whose buyers think before they buy. That includes clinics and hospitals, law and accounting firms, consultancies, schools and edtech companies, software and SaaS brands, real estate developers, travel companies, manufacturers selling to other businesses, and online stores with a real point of difference.

We work with founders and in-house marketing teams alike. You can hand us the whole programme, or bring us in for one piece, such as a technical audit, a GEO baseline or a website rebuild, and keep the rest in-house.

## Questions about AVENZA {wide}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is AVENZA a full-service agency?
We cover the channels that decide whether a business is found and chosen online: AI visibility, SEO, content, digital PR, Google Ads, social media, websites and conversion. We do not offer offline media buying or event management.

? Do you work with small businesses?
Yes, when there is a clear goal and a realistic budget for it. For smaller businesses we often start with one focused service, such as local SEO or a website rebuild, and expand once it pays for itself.

? Do you work with clients outside India?
Yes. We work remotely with clients in other countries and plan around their time zone for calls.

? Can we hire you for a one-time project?
Yes. Audits, website builds, migrations and GEO baselines are often one-time projects. Ongoing services such as SEO and paid media work best on a monthly basis.
:::
