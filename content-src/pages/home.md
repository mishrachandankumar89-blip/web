## Search has two front doors now

> The ten blue links are still there. Above them, an answer is now written for your buyer before they click anything.

That is why we built AVENZA around one idea: **be the answer, not just a result.** Rankings still matter. Being the source an answer is built on matters more.

## The AVENZA visibility stack

> AI visibility is the top layer of a stack. It only holds if everything underneath it is solid.

Most agencies jump straight to the tactic of the month. We work from the bottom up. A site that crawlers cannot read will never be cited. Content without evidence will not be trusted. A brand nobody else mentions has nothing to confirm it.

![Diagram of five stacked layers from technical foundation to AI visibility](img:diagram-stack "The five layers we build, from the foundation up.")

- **Technical foundation:** crawlable, indexable, fast pages with structured data.
- **Content and entities:** pages that cover each topic fully, with clear facts about your business.
- **Authority and mentions:** links, reviews and coverage on sites people and AI tools trust.
- **Answer formatting:** direct, quotable answers, tables and FAQs.
- **AI visibility:** consistent citations in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and AI Overviews.

## Questions we hear first {wide}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?
SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO shapes your content so search engines and voice assistants can lift a direct answer from it. GEO works to get your brand cited inside AI-written answers from tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. They build on each other, and we plan them together.

? Do we still need SEO if people use AI assistants?
Yes. Google says pages must be indexed and eligible for a snippet to appear as links in AI Overviews and AI Mode, and most assistants search the web before they answer. Strong SEO is what gets you into the pool of sources AI tools choose from.

? Which businesses do you work with?
Businesses whose buyers research before they decide: professional services, healthcare, education, software, e-commerce, real estate, travel and premium consumer brands, in India and abroad.

? How soon will we see results?
Technical fixes and paid campaigns can show results within weeks. SEO and AI visibility usually build over four to twelve months. We set expectations for your market after the first audit.

? How do we start?
Book a strategy call or send a WhatsApp message. We will ask about your goals, review your site and competitors, and propose a clear first ninety days.
