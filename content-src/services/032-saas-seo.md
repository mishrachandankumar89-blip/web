## What SaaS SEO covers

> SaaS buyers often discover tools through searches like 'how to automate invoices' long before they search for a product name.

We find those problem searches, create content that solves them with your product as part of the answer, and build scalable page types for integrations, use cases and alternatives. Sign-ups and demo requests are tracked back to each page.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.
