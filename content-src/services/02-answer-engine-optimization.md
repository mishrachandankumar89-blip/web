## Why answers matter more than rankings now

> People no longer search in two-word fragments. They ask full questions and expect a reply.

Google introduced featured snippets years ago so that a results page could show an answer above the links. People Also Ask boxes followed, then voice assistants, then AI Overviews. Each step moved the answer closer to the searcher and further from the click.

The pattern is the same everywhere: a system reads many pages, picks the clearest reply, and shows it with a credit. The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that 44 percent of Indian internet users had already used AI-enabled features such as voice search, image search or chatbots. Those features do not show ten options. They show one answer.

AEO is how you become that answer. It is less about tricks and more about editorial discipline: knowing the question, answering it in the first two sentences, and then proving it.

## How answer engines choose a reply

No search engine publishes its full method, but the documented signals and years of testing point to a consistent set of traits in the pages that get picked:

- **The answer comes first.** A 40 to 60 word reply right below a heading that matches the question.
- **The page is specific.** Numbers, names, steps and conditions beat general statements.
- **Structure is visible.** Lists for steps, tables for comparisons, short paragraphs for definitions.
- **The source is credible.** Clear authorship, a real business behind it and supporting references.
- **The page is technically eligible.** Indexed, crawlable and not blocked from showing a snippet.

That last point is easy to miss. Google's documentation says a page must be eligible to show a snippet to appear in AI features. A stray `nosnippet` rule or an over-tight `max-snippet` setting can quietly remove a page from contention.

::: note
FAQ markup is no longer a shortcut. In August 2023 Google limited FAQ rich results to well-known, authoritative government and health websites, and it retired How-to rich results soon after. We still use FAQPage markup because it helps machines read the page, but the visible answer is what wins.
:::

## What AEO work includes {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Question research.** We mine Search Console, People Also Ask, sales and support conversations, Reddit, Quora and AI tools to build a ranked list of questions per service.
- **Answer-first rewrites.** Each priority page gets a direct answer block, a clear H2 for every question and supporting detail below it.
- **Snippet formatting.** Definitions as one paragraph, processes as numbered steps, comparisons as tables, so each format matches the answer type.
- **Structured data.** Organization, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList and Article markup that matches what is visible on the page.
- **Voice and local readiness.** Spoken-style answers, consistent business details and Google Business Profile Q and A for local intent.
- **Snippet tracking.** Monthly tracking of featured snippets, People Also Ask and AI Overview links won and lost.
:::

## Our AEO method {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Collect the questions.** Real phrasing from buyers, not keyword tools alone, grouped by intent and buying stage.
- **Audit current answers.** For each question we record who answers it today, in which format, and how well.
- **Write the best reply.** A direct answer first, then evidence, examples and limits, in plain language.
- **Format for extraction.** Headings that mirror the question, lists, tables and markup that match the answer type.
- **Track and refine.** Monthly checks of snippets and AI answers, with rewrites where a competitor still wins.
:::

## Writing answers machines can quote

> The best AEO copy reads like an expert talking to one person, not like a page written for robots.

A good answer block does four things. It restates the question in plain words, gives the answer in the first sentence, adds the one condition that matters, and points to where the detail lives. For example, a clinic answering "How long does a root canal take?" should give the typical time range first, then explain what makes it longer, then link to the treatment page.

We avoid the habits that make AI-era content worse: padding the opening, repeating the keyword, and hiding the answer behind a long story. We also keep each answer factual and dated where facts change, because assistants prefer current sources.

| Question type | Best format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What is | One paragraph definition, 40 to 60 words | What is GEO? |
| How to | Numbered steps with a one-line summary first | How to set up Google Business Profile |
| Which is better | Comparison table with a verdict | SEO vs Google Ads for a new clinic |
| How much | Range, what drives cost, and an example | How much does a website cost in India? |
| Is it worth it | Direct yes or no with conditions | Is local SEO worth it for a single shop? |

## How we measure AEO

Answer wins are visible, which makes AEO one of the easier channels to report on. Every month we show featured snippets held, People Also Ask placements, AI Overview and AI Mode links, and the questions where a competitor still wins. In Google Analytics 4 we track the visits and enquiries from those pages, and in Search Console we watch impressions on question queries, which often rise before clicks do.

Expect early snippet wins within weeks on pages that already rank on page one, because the change is mostly formatting. Questions where you do not rank yet take longer and usually need [SEO](service:seo-services) and [content](service:content-marketing) work alongside.

## Questions about AEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What is the difference between AEO and GEO?
AEO focuses on answer features such as featured snippets, People Also Ask and voice replies. GEO focuses on being cited in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews. The skills overlap, and we usually run them together.

? Does FAQ schema still help?
FAQ rich results now show mainly for authoritative government and health sites. The markup still helps machines understand question and answer pairs, so we add it where the questions are real and visible on the page. It is a support, not a strategy.

? Will featured snippets reduce my clicks?
Sometimes a snippet answers the question fully and the person does not click. For questions that lead to a purchase, the brand credit and the trust it builds usually outweigh that. We prioritise questions where being the answer leads to an enquiry.

? How many questions should a page answer?
One main question, plus the follow-ups people ask next. A service page might answer five to eight. Stuffing dozens of loosely related questions onto one page makes every answer weaker.
:::
