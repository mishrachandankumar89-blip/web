## What Amazon Ads Management covers

> Amazon advertising works best when paid and organic are managed together, because ad sales help organic ranking.

We set target advertising cost of sales by product, harvest converting search terms into manual campaigns, protect your brand terms, and review bids weekly. Reporting shows total and organic sales, not just ad-attributed revenue.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## Questions about Amazon Ads Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What is ACoS?
Advertising cost of sales: ad spend divided by ad-attributed sales, shown as a percentage.

? Do Amazon ads help organic rankings?
Sales from ads can support organic rank, which is why we track both together.

? Do you manage Flipkart ads too?
Yes. Marketplace advertising on Flipkart is part of our marketplace services.
:::
