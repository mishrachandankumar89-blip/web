## What LinkedIn B2B Marketing covers

> On LinkedIn, people follow people. Leadership profiles usually reach far more buyers than company pages.

We interview your leaders, write posts in their voice for their approval, run the company page calendar, create document carousels and newsletters, and set a simple engagement routine so posts reach the right people.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.
