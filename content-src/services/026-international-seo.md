## What Multilingual and International SEO covers

> The most common international SEO problem is the wrong page ranking in the wrong country, for example a US price page appearing for Indian searchers.

We plan the structure with you, whether subfolders, subdomains or country domains, implement and test hreflang, and research keywords in each market and language. For India, that often includes Hindi and regional language pages for buyers who search that way.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Multilingual and International SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is machine translation enough?
For draft work it helps, but pages that rank and convert need human review for terms, tone and local detail.

? Should we use subfolders or country domains?
Subfolders are simpler to manage and build authority in one place. Country domains can make sense for strong local brands. We recommend case by case.

? Do you create Hindi content?
Yes, where your buyers search in Hindi or other Indian languages, with proper hreflang setup.
:::
