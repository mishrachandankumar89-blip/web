## What AI Crawler and llms.txt Setup covers

> One-click bot blocking at the CDN, an old robots.txt rule or an overzealous security plugin can silently remove a site from AI answers.

We review every layer that decides which crawlers reach your pages, agree a policy with you for search, training and user-triggered bots, implement it, and add an llms.txt file that describes your key pages accurately. Then we confirm in server logs that the right crawlers are arriving.

## Questions about AI Crawler and llms.txt Setup {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is llms.txt a ranking factor?
No. It is a proposed standard. Google's May 2026 guide says it gets no special treatment in Search. It is cheap to add and may help some tools.

? Does robots.txt protect private content?
No. It asks reputable crawlers to stay away but is not a security control. Private content belongs behind a login.

? Will allowing AI crawlers slow our site?
Rarely. If a crawler strains your server, rate limiting at the server or CDN is more reliable than crawl-delay rules.
:::
