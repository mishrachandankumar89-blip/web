## What On-Page SEO covers

> Pages ranking between positions 5 and 20 are usually the quickest wins in any SEO programme.

We review each priority page against the results that outrank it: what they cover, how they answer, how they are structured. Then we rewrite and restructure your page to serve the search better, without stuffing keywords or changing what makes it yours.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Intent check.** Does the page match what searchers actually want?
- **Title and meta rewrite.** Clear, click-worthy, within length limits.
- **Structure and answers.** Logical headings and a direct answer near the top.
- **Internal links.** Links in and out with descriptive anchor text.
- **Images and markup.** Alt text, sizing and structured data that matches the page.

:::

## Questions about On-Page SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How soon do on-page changes show results?
Often within a few weeks of Google recrawling the page, especially for pages already on page one or two.

? Will you change our brand voice?
No. We keep your voice and improve structure, clarity and coverage.

? Do meta descriptions affect rankings?
Not directly, but a clear description can raise click-through rate, which brings more visits from the same ranking.
:::
