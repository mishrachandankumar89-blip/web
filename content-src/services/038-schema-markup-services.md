## What Schema Markup Services covers

> Markup that does not match the page, or that is copied from a template without thought, can be ignored or even trigger a manual action.

We decide which schema types genuinely fit each template, implement them in JSON-LD, connect them into one consistent graph with stable IDs, validate them with Google's tools and monitor errors in Search Console.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Schema Markup Services {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Does schema markup improve rankings?
Not directly. It helps search engines understand pages and can make them eligible for rich results, which can improve visibility and clicks.

? Do FAQ rich results still show?
Since August 2023 Google shows FAQ rich results mainly for well-known government and health sites. The markup can still help machines read the page.

? Can schema be added to any CMS?
Yes. We implement it through themes, plugins, tag management or code, depending on your setup.
:::
