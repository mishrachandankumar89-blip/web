## What Checkout Optimization covers

> Baymard Institute's average across 50 studies puts cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Much of it comes from fixable friction.

We analyse where shoppers drop out, review your checkout against usability research, fix the biggest issues, test changes where traffic allows, and set up cart recovery messages by email and WhatsApp with consent.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

## Questions about Checkout Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Should we force account creation?
Usually not. Guest checkout reduces abandonment; offer an account after purchase.

? Does cash on delivery still matter in India?
For many categories, yes. Offering it alongside UPI and cards can lift completion.

? Can you work on Shopify checkout?
Yes, within Shopify's checkout customisation options and apps.
:::
