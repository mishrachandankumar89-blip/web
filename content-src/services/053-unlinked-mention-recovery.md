## What Unlinked Mention Recovery covers

> Mentions without links still help brand recognition, but a link makes the connection explicit for readers and search engines.

We monitor the web for mentions of your brand name, products, executives and research, filter for relevant, reputable sites, and send short, friendly requests. We also correct wrong details, such as an old address, when we find them.

Google says local prominence draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in. Its spam policies name link schemes, including bought links that pass ranking credit, as a reason to demote a site. Earned authority is the only kind that lasts.

## Questions about Unlinked Mention Recovery {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is this worth it for small brands?
It scales with how often you are mentioned. Brands with press coverage or active founders usually see steady wins.

? Will site owners be annoyed?
Not if requests are polite and relevant. We contact each site once with a clear, friendly note.

Yes. Correcting facts at their source helps AI assistants describe you accurately.
