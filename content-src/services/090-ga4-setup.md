## What Google Analytics 4 Setup and Audit covers

> Many GA4 properties count page views as conversions, double-count forms or lose sales through payment gateways.

We audit your setup, define a measurement plan with you, implement events through Google Tag Manager, test them, and build simple reports your team will actually use.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

## Questions about Google Analytics 4 Setup and Audit {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What are key events?
Key events are the GA4 events you mark as important to your business, such as form submissions or purchases.

? Can you fix historical data?
Past data cannot usually be corrected, but everything from the fix onward will be accurate.

? Do we need consent mode?
It depends on your markets and audience. We advise on consent settings that suit your legal obligations.
:::
