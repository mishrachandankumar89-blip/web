## What AI Visibility Audit covers

> Most brands have never systematically checked what AI tools say about them. The results are often surprising: outdated prices, wrong locations, or competitors recommended for your own speciality.

The audit gives you a clear baseline: your share of citations by assistant and topic, the competitors that win, the sources AI tools trust in your space, and the technical issues that might be keeping crawlers out. It ends with a 90-day action plan.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Question set.** 50 to 200 real buyer questions agreed with you.
- **Multi-assistant testing.** ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI features.
- **Citation share report.** You against named competitors, by topic.
- **Accuracy review.** Wrong or outdated statements about your business, with sources.
- **90-day action plan.** Fixes ranked by impact and effort.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Question map.** The buyer questions that matter, grouped by stage and value.
- **Baseline.** Each question run across the main assistants, with every brand and source cited.
- **Foundation.** Crawler access, indexing, structured data and one set of brand facts.
- **Evidence.** Answer-first pages, original data and expert input that deserve citation.
- **Track.** The same questions re-run monthly, reported as share of citations.
:::

## Questions about AI Visibility Audit {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How long does the audit take?
Usually two to three weeks, depending on the number of questions and markets.

? Is it a one-time project?
It can be. Many businesses then re-run the same question set quarterly to track progress.

? Why do results vary between runs?
AI answers change with wording, location and time. We use many questions and repeat runs so patterns, not single answers, drive decisions.
