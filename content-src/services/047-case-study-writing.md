## What Case Study Writing covers

> Case studies only work when they are true, specific and approved by the client. We never invent results.

We interview your team and, with permission, your client, verify every figure, and write a clear story with a headline result. Each case study is formatted for the website, for sales decks and as a downloadable PDF.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Brief.** Search intent, questions to answer, sources and the expert to interview.
- **Interview.** A short call to capture real experience and examples.
- **Draft.** Answer first, evidence after, sources linked.
- **Edit and fact-check.** Every claim checked, every number dated.
- **Publish and refresh.** Performance reviewed after 90 days, then updated.
:::

## Questions about Case Study Writing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What if the client wants to stay anonymous?
We can write an anonymised case study with the client's approval, keeping the details accurate.

? Do you need exact numbers?
Approved numbers make case studies far stronger, but ranges or relative improvements work when exact figures are confidential.

? How long does one take?
Usually two to three weeks, mostly waiting for interviews and approvals.
:::
