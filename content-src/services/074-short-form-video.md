## What Short-Form Video Production covers

> The IAMAI and Kantar 2025 report found around 588 million Indian internet users watch short-video content. Attention is there, but only for videos that earn it.

We plan topics, write scripts with strong hooks, give your team simple shoot plans, and edit with captions, cuts and music. Retention data from each batch shapes the next one.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

## Questions about Short-Form Video Production {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can we film on a phone?
Yes. Modern phones are more than good enough. Light and sound matter more than the camera.

? How many videos per month?
Eight to twelve is a strong start for most brands, filmed in one or two batches.

? Should we follow trends?
Sometimes. Trends help reach, but videos that answer real customer questions build more trust.
:::
