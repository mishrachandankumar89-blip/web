## What PPC Audit covers

> Most accounts we audit lose money in a few predictable places: broken tracking, broad targeting without negatives, and ads sending traffic to generic pages.

With read-only access, we review your account in depth, quantify waste where we can, and explain each issue in plain language. You can implement the changes yourself or ask us to.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Tracking check.** Conversions, values and duplicates verified.
- **Structure review.** Campaigns, ad groups and match types.
- **Waste analysis.** Search terms and placements with no return.
- **Bidding and budget.** Strategies and splits matched to goals.
- **Action plan.** Fixes ranked by impact, with estimated savings.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Track first.** No budget increase until conversions are measured accurately.
- **Structure for intent.** Brand, high-intent and research campaigns kept apart.
- **Write to the search.** Ads and landing pages that answer the exact query.
- **Prune weekly.** Search terms and placements reviewed, waste excluded.
- **Scale what works.** Budget moved to campaigns with proven returns.
:::
