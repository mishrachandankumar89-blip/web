## What Instagram Marketing covers

> Instagram rewards content people save, share and watch through. Polished but empty posts rarely do that.

We define your content pillars, plan a monthly calendar, script Reels your team can film quickly, design carousels and stories, and manage comments and messages. The best organic posts get paid support.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

## Questions about Instagram Marketing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How often should we post on Instagram?
Three to five quality posts a week plus regular stories is a strong rhythm for most brands.

? Do hashtags still matter?
They help a little with discovery. Content quality and watch time matter far more.

? Can Instagram work for B2B?
Yes, especially for showing team culture, projects and expertise, though LinkedIn usually leads for B2B.
:::
