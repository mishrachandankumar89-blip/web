## What a website has to do now

> A beautiful site that loads slowly, confuses visitors or hides its content from crawlers is an expensive brochure.

Visitors decide quickly. A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed increased retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. Speed is not a technical vanity metric; it is revenue.

Search engines and AI assistants are also visitors. They need content in clean HTML, clear headings, descriptive links, structured data and pages that do not hide text behind heavy scripts. A site built with this in mind starts ranking sooner and is easier for AI tools to quote.

And every site must work for everyone. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, is the current accessibility standard. It covers contrast, keyboard use, focus visibility and minimum target sizes, among much else. Accessible sites are better for every user and reduce legal risk in many markets.

## How we design

### Strategy before pixels

We start with your buyers, your offers and the questions people ask before they contact you. That shapes the sitemap, the page structure and the copy before any visual design begins.

### Design that signals quality

Typography, spacing, colour and imagery chosen for your market. A law firm and a skincare brand should not look alike. We design mobile layouts first, because that is where most visits start, then scale up to desktop.

### Content that converts

Clear headlines, proof near every claim, answer-first sections and a call to action that matches what the visitor is ready for. We write the copy or work from yours.

## How we build

- **WordPress with native blocks**, so your team edits pages in the normal editor without a heavy page builder.
- **WooCommerce or Shopify** for stores, chosen by catalogue size, payments and how you sell.
- **Custom builds** on modern frameworks when the product needs it.
- **Performance budgets**: modern image formats, fonts loaded carefully, minimal JavaScript and no layout shift.
- **SEO and AI foundations**: clean URLs, one H1 per page, meta tags, Organization, Service and FAQPage schema, XML sitemap, robots rules and an llms.txt file.
- **Accessibility checks**: contrast, keyboard paths, focus states, alt text and tap targets tested before launch.
- **Security and care**: SSL, backups, updates and uptime monitoring after launch.

## Redesigning without losing rankings

Redesigns and platform moves are where many businesses lose years of SEO progress overnight. URLs change without redirects, content is trimmed, internal links break and tracking stops. We treat migration as its own workstream: every old URL is mapped, redirects are tested on staging, metadata and structured data carry over, and rankings and indexing are watched daily after launch.

| Risk in a redesign | How we prevent it |
|---|---|
| Changed URLs without redirects | Full URL map and 301 redirects tested before launch |
| Content removed during simplification | Page-by-page content inventory with traffic data |
| Tracking lost | GA4 and conversion events tested on staging and live |
| Slower new design | Performance budget agreed before design starts |
| Robots or noindex left from staging | Launch checklist with crawl verification |

## Questions about website projects {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How long does a new website take?
A focused business site of ten to twenty pages typically takes six to ten weeks from discovery to launch. Larger sites, stores and custom builds take longer. We agree on a timeline with milestones before work starts.

? Will we be able to edit the site ourselves?
Yes. We build with native WordPress blocks or your platform's standard editor, and we train your team before handover.

? Do you write the content?
We can write all of it, edit what you have, or combine both. Content is usually the step that delays launches, so we plan it from day one.

? What happens after launch?
We offer care plans for updates, backups, security, monitoring and small changes, and we can continue with SEO, content and campaigns.
:::
