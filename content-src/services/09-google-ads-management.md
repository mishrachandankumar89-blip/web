## Why paid search works, and why it often does not

> Google Ads is an auction where the best-prepared advertiser often pays less, not more.

Google ranks ads using bid, the quality of the ad and landing page, and the expected impact of extensions and formats. Its Quality Score diagnostic, rated from 1 to 10, reflects three things: expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience, each compared with other advertisers on the same keyword over the last 90 days. Accounts that score well usually pay less for each click and win better positions.

Most accounts we audit lose money in the same few places. Broad match keywords run without enough negative keywords, so ads show for searches that will never buy. Conversion tracking counts page views or duplicate form submits, so the bidding system optimises toward the wrong thing. And ads send people to a generic home page instead of a page that answers the exact search.

Fix those three and the same budget usually goes noticeably further.

## Campaign types we run

### Search

The core of most accounts: ads that appear when someone searches for what you sell. We build tightly themed ad groups, responsive search ads with strong headlines, and sitelinks, callouts and call extensions that take up more space and answer objections early.

### Performance Max

Google's goal-based campaign type that runs across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps from one budget. It can work very well when it is fed accurate conversion data, good creative and clear audience signals. Without those, it spends on placements you would never choose. We set it up with care and watch its reports closely.

### Shopping

Product listing ads from your Merchant Center feed, organised by margin and performance so best sellers get the budget they deserve.

### YouTube and Demand Gen

Video and visual campaigns to build demand before people search, retarget visitors and reach audiences similar to your best customers.

## How we manage an account {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Track first.** No spend increases until conversions are measured accurately.
- **Structure for intent.** Separate campaigns for brand, high-intent and research searches.
- **Write to the search.** Ads and landing pages that answer the exact query.
- **Prune weekly.** Search terms reviewed and irrelevant ones excluded.
- **Test steadily.** One clear test at a time on ads, pages and bidding.
- **Scale what works.** Budget moved to the campaigns with proven returns.
:::

## Paid and organic together

Google Ads and SEO are stronger together. Paid search shows you within days which searches convert, which tells us where to invest in SEO content. As organic rankings grow, we can reduce spend on searches you already win for free and move budget to new opportunities. For local businesses, Local Services Ads and call-focused campaigns often pair well with [Local SEO](service:local-seo).

| Goal | Campaign mix we often start with |
|---|---|
| Leads for a service business | Search with call extensions, then Performance Max once tracking is solid |
| Online store sales | Shopping or Performance Max with a clean feed, plus brand Search |
| New product awareness | YouTube and Demand Gen, with Search to capture the demand it creates |
| Local footfall | Search with location targeting and Maps placements |

## Questions about Google Ads {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How much should we spend on Google Ads?
Enough to collect useful data. In most markets that means a budget that can buy at least a few dozen clicks a day on your main searches. We estimate costs from Google's Keyword Planner and competitor research before you commit.

? How are management fees and ad spend handled?
Your ad spend is billed by Google directly to your own account, so you always see exactly what Google charged. Our management fee is quoted separately, after we understand how many campaigns, markets and landing pages the work covers.

? Who owns the ad account?
You do. We work inside your account with manager access, and all data, history and conversions stay with you if we ever part ways.

? How soon will we see results?
Ads can bring enquiries within days. The first four to six weeks are a learning period while bidding systems gather data and we remove waste. Steady improvement comes after that.
:::
