## Why AI answers now decide who gets the customer

> Search has not disappeared. It has grown a second front door, and that door talks back.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Sundar Pichai said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and that AI Mode, Google's conversational search, had passed one billion monthly users within a year of launch. In the same year OpenAI said ChatGPT had passed one billion weekly users. India is not on the edges of this shift: Sam Altman has said India is ChatGPT's second-largest market, with more than 100 million weekly users.

What changes for a business is the shape of the result. A classic results page shows ten links and lets the buyer choose. An AI answer reads those pages for the buyer, writes one reply and names a handful of sources. If your brand is not among them, you were never in the room.

Pew Research Center measured this in 2025. When a Google results page showed an AI summary, users clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits, against 15 percent when no summary appeared. They clicked a link inside the summary on just 1 percent of visits. Being the source the answer is built on is now worth more than ranking just below it.

::: note
GEO does not replace SEO. Google says pages need to be indexed and eligible for a snippet to appear as a link in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Most AI assistants also search the web before they answer. Strong SEO is the entry ticket; GEO is how you get chosen once you are in.
:::

## What the research says actually moves AI visibility

The term Generative Engine Optimization comes from a 2023 paper by researchers at Princeton, Georgia Tech, the Allen Institute for AI and IIT Delhi, later presented at KDD 2024, one of the main data science conferences. The team built a benchmark of around 10,000 queries and tested nine ways of rewriting a source page to see which ones made generative engines use and cite it more.

Three findings shape how we work:

- **Evidence wins.** Adding relevant statistics, quotations from credible people and citations to reliable sources raised visibility the most, by up to 40 percent in the paper's tests.
- **Keyword stuffing does not help.** The tactic that once gamed old search engines performed poorly in generative answers.
- **Results depend on the domain.** What works for a legal question differs from what works for a product comparison, so every plan starts with your own buyer questions, not a template.

Google's own guidance points the same way. Its page on AI features says there are no special tricks, and its May 2026 guide to generative AI search calls for valuable, unique, non-commodity content and says SEO best practices remain relevant because these features are rooted in its core ranking systems.

## What a GEO engagement includes {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

> Every item below is a concrete output you can see, not a vague promise of "AI optimization".

::: grid 3
- **AI visibility audit.** We run 50 to 200 real buyer questions through ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google, and record who is cited, how often and with what claims.
- **Entity and fact clean-up.** One consistent description of your company, services, people and locations across your site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories and Wikidata where it fits.
- **Answer-first page rewrites.** Key pages restructured so each one opens with a direct, quotable answer, followed by evidence, tables and specifics a model can lift cleanly.
- **Original evidence assets.** Data studies, pricing explainers, comparison tables and expert commentary that give AI systems a reason to cite you rather than a competitor.
- **Technical access for AI crawlers.** robots.txt rules for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and others, structured data, an llms.txt file and fast, crawlable pages.
- **Mentions on trusted sites.** Digital PR, expert quotes and listings on the publications and review sites that AI tools already lean on in your category.
:::

## How we run GEO, step by step {wide}

^ The process

> A GEO programme runs in monthly cycles. The first ninety days build the base; after that, each month adds evidence and measures what changed.

::: steps
- **Question map.** We collect the questions your buyers actually ask, from sales calls, search data, People Also Ask boxes and forums, and group them by stage and value.
- **Baseline.** Each question is run across the major assistants. We log which brands are named, which pages are cited and what the answers get wrong about you.
- **Fix the foundation.** Crawl access, indexing, structured data, site speed and a single source of truth for your company facts.
- **Build citable pages.** We rewrite or create the pages that should answer each question, with sources, data and clear structure.
- **Earn outside proof.** Mentions, reviews and expert quotes on sites that AI systems already trust for your topic.
- **Measure and repeat.** The same questions are re-run every month, so you see share of citations move, not just traffic.
:::

## What results to expect, and when

> GEO compounds slowly and then quickly. Anyone who promises AI citations in two weeks is guessing.

Assistants that search the web live, such as Perplexity, ChatGPT search and Google AI Mode, can start using an improved page within days of it being crawled and indexed. Answers that lean on what a model learned during training move far more slowly, because they only change when the model is retrained.

| Period | What usually happens | What we report |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1 to 4 | Audit, question map, technical fixes, first page rewrites | Baseline share of citations by assistant and topic |
| Months 2 to 3 | Rewritten pages indexed, first new evidence assets live | Pages cited, questions where you now appear, errors corrected |
| Months 4 to 6 | Mentions on outside sites start to count, more pages cited | Share of citations against named competitors |
| Month 6 onward | Steady gains on priority questions, new topics added | Trend lines, leads from AI referrals in GA4 |

We do not guarantee placements. No one controls what an AI model writes. What we can do is make your brand the easiest, best-supported answer to cite, and show you every month whether that is working.

## Who GEO is right for

GEO pays off fastest for businesses whose buyers research before they contact anyone. That includes B2B services, software, healthcare providers, education, financial and legal services, travel, real estate and premium consumer brands.

It is less urgent if most of your sales come from walk-in traffic or a marketplace listing. In that case we will usually recommend [Local SEO](service:local-seo) or [E-commerce SEO](service:ecommerce-seo) first, and add GEO once the basics are in place.

- You sell something people compare before buying
- Your competitors already show up in AI answers and you do not
- AI tools describe your company wrongly or out of date
- You publish expertise but rarely get credit for it

## Questions about GEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is GEO different from SEO?
Yes, but they overlap heavily. SEO earns rankings in search results. GEO earns mentions and citations inside AI-written answers. Both need crawlable, trustworthy pages. GEO puts extra weight on quotable facts, original evidence, consistent brand information and mentions on other trusted sites.

? Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?
No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. AI answers vary by question, by user and by day. We measure your share of citations across a fixed set of questions every month, so progress is visible and honest.

? Do we need an llms.txt file?
It helps some tools and costs little, so we set one up. It is a proposed standard, not a ranking factor, and Google's May 2026 guide says llms.txt gets no special treatment in Search. Clean pages, open crawler access and structured data matter far more.

? How do you measure AI visibility?
Three ways. Search Console's generative AI performance report, launched by Google in June 2026, shows how often your pages appear as links in AI Overviews and AI Mode. Google Analytics 4 shows visits from chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com and copilot.microsoft.com. And our monthly question tracking shows your share of citations against competitors, which matters because many AI answers produce no click at all.

? Should we block AI crawlers to protect our content?
That is a business decision. Blocking training crawlers keeps your text out of future models but can also keep your brand out of their answers. We usually allow search and answer crawlers and decide on training crawlers case by case.
:::
