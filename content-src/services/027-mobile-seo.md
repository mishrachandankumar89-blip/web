## What Mobile SEO covers

> If your mobile page hides content that exists on desktop, Google may never see it, because it ranks the mobile version.

We test your key templates on real devices and emulated slow networks, compare mobile and desktop content, check Core Web Vitals field data for phones, and fix the layout, speed and usability problems that hurt both rankings and conversion.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Mobile SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What is mobile-first indexing?
Google mainly uses the mobile version of a page's content for indexing and ranking.

? Is a responsive design enough?
It is the right foundation, but speed, tap targets and content parity still need checking.

? Do pop-ups hurt mobile SEO?
Intrusive interstitials that block content on mobile can harm the experience and rankings. Small, easy-to-dismiss banners are safer.
:::
