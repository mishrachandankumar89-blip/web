answer_q: What is the difference between UI and UX design?
answer: UX design shapes how a product works for people: their goals, the journeys they take, the structure of information and how easy each task is. UI design shapes how it looks and feels: layout, typography, colour, components and states. Good digital products need both, informed by research and tested with real users.

## What UI and UX Design covers

> Beautiful screens that confuse users still fail. Research and testing keep design honest.

We start with user research and analytics, map key journeys, test wireframes early, then create visual designs and a reusable design system. Prototypes are tested with real users before development begins.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.
