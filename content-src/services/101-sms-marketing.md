## What SMS Marketing covers

> SMS is powerful because it is personal, which is also why misuse quickly annoys people.

We handle DLT registration, sender IDs and templates with your SMS provider, set up consent and segmentation, and plan messages that customers find useful, tracked with short links.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.

## Questions about SMS Marketing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? What is DLT?
Distributed Ledger Technology registration, required by TRAI for businesses sending commercial SMS in India.

? Can we send promotional SMS at any time?
No. Promotional messages have timing restrictions and must respect customer preferences.

? Is SMS better than WhatsApp?
They suit different uses. SMS works for short reminders; WhatsApp for richer conversations.
:::
