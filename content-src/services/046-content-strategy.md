## What Content Strategy covers

> Without a strategy, teams publish what is easy rather than what moves the business.

We audit what you already have, map the questions your buyers ask at each stage, organise them into topic clusters, and build a realistic calendar for your team's capacity. Measurement is agreed up front so content earns its budget.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Content audit.** What to keep, update, merge or remove.
- **Audience and question map.** Topics by persona and buying stage.
- **Topic clusters.** Pillar pages and supporting articles linked logically.
- **Editorial calendar.** Twelve weeks of planned pieces with owners.
- **Measurement plan.** KPIs from traffic to leads and AI citations.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Brief.** Search intent, questions to answer, sources and the expert to interview.
- **Interview.** A short call to capture real experience and examples.
- **Draft.** Answer first, evidence after, sources linked.
- **Edit and fact-check.** Every claim checked, every number dated.
- **Publish and refresh.** Performance reviewed after 90 days, then updated.
:::

## Questions about Content Strategy {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How long does a strategy take?
Typically three to five weeks, depending on the size of your existing content.

? Can our team run it afterwards?
Yes. We design the plan around your team's capacity and hand over templates.

? Should we delete old content?
Sometimes. Thin or outdated pages are often better merged into stronger ones or redirected.
:::
