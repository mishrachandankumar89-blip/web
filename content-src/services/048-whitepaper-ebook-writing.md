## What Whitepaper and Ebook Writing covers

> The best gated content is something your buyers would gladly share their email for, and recommend to a colleague.

We choose a topic with your sales team, research it properly, interview your experts, then write and design the document. We also write the landing page, follow-up emails and social posts to promote it.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

## Questions about Whitepaper and Ebook Writing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Should we gate it behind a form?
It depends on your goal. Gating gathers leads; an open version earns more links and AI citations. Some brands do both.

? How long should an ebook be?
Long enough to be genuinely useful, often 2,000 to 6,000 words, with visuals.

? Can it be repurposed?
Yes. Chapters become articles, posts and webinar topics.
:::
