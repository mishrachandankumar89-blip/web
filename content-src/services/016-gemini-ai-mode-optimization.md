## What Gemini and AI Mode Optimization covers

> AI Mode handles complex questions by running many related searches and combining the results, so depth across subtopics and accurate structured data both count.

We map the long, conversational questions your buyers ask, check which sources Google's AI cites for them, and fill the gaps on your site. For local businesses we tighten Business Profile data; for stores we clean product feeds, since Google's shopping answers draw on them.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

## Questions about Gemini and AI Mode Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is AI Mode available in India?
Yes. Google launched AI Mode in India through Search Labs in June 2025, then opened it to all users and later added Hindi and other Indian languages.

? Do we need special AI markup?
No. Google's May 2026 guide says there is no special markup for AI features. Standard structured data that matches visible content is enough.

? Does Gemini use the same sources as Search?
Google says its generative AI features in Search are rooted in its core ranking and quality systems, so strong Search visibility is the foundation.
:::
