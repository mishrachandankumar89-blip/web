## What Google Penalty Recovery covers

> Not every drop is a penalty. Many are caused by technical changes, tracking errors or competitors improving.

We separate the possible causes using Search Console, analytics, crawl history and update timelines. Then we build a recovery plan: technical fixes, content pruning or improvement, link clean-up if needed, and a well-documented reconsideration request for manual actions.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Google Penalty Recovery {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How do I know if I have a manual action?
Google Search Console shows manual actions in its Security and Manual Actions section.

? Should we disavow links?
Only when there is a link-related manual action or clear evidence of harmful link schemes. Google usually ignores low-quality links on its own.

? How long does recovery take?
Manual action reviews can take weeks. Recovery from core updates often shows after a later update, once quality has improved.
:::
