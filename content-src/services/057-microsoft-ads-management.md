## What Microsoft Ads Management covers

> Many advertisers import their Google campaigns into Microsoft Ads once and never look again, leaving money on the table.

We import carefully, then adjust bids, keywords and audiences for Microsoft's traffic, add LinkedIn profile targeting for B2B, set up conversion tracking with UET, and review search terms weekly like any other account.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## Questions about Microsoft Ads Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is Bing traffic lower quality?
Not necessarily. It often skews toward desktop and professional users, which suits many B2B advertisers.

? Can we just import our Google campaigns?
Importing is a good start, but bids, budgets and audiences need their own tuning.

? What is UET?
Universal Event Tracking is Microsoft's conversion tag, similar to the Google tag.
