## What Google Shopping Ads covers

> In Shopping, the product feed does the job keywords do in Search. A weak feed means weak targeting.

We fix feed errors, improve titles and attributes, add custom labels for margin and stock, and split campaigns so budget follows profit. Search terms are reviewed weekly to cut waste.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## Questions about Google Shopping Ads {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Do Shopping ads need a Merchant Center account?
Yes. Products come from your Merchant Center feed, linked to Google Ads.

? Should we also use free listings?
Yes. Free listings use the same feed and can add sales at no cost per click.

? How do you handle out-of-stock items?
The feed is kept in sync so out-of-stock products stop spending budget.
:::
