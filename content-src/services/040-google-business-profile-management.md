## What Google Business Profile Management covers

> Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and a complete, active profile helps with the first and the last.

We take care of the profile every month: posts, photos, offers, holiday hours, review replies and checks for unwanted edits. We also report on calls, direction requests and website clicks so you can see what the profile brings in.

## Questions about Google Business Profile Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can you fix a suspended profile?
We can diagnose the likely cause, correct it and guide the reinstatement request through Google's process.

? Should we add keywords to our business name?
No. Google's guidelines require your real business name, and keyword stuffing can lead to suspension.

? How often should we post?
Weekly is a good rhythm. Regular posts and photos show an active business.
