## What Flipkart Marketplace Management covers

> Flipkart's sale events can deliver a big share of yearly sales, but only for sellers who prepare their catalogue and budgets early.

We improve your listings, manage Flipkart Ads, monitor seller metrics and plan for major sale events, with reporting on sales, returns and advertising return.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, and Baymard Institute puts average online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Reach is large, but so is the drop-off, which is why clean listings, accurate feeds and a smooth checkout matter as much as traffic.
