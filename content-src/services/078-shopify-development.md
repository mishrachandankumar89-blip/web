## What Shopify Development covers

> Shopify is reliable and fast to launch, but many stores become slow and cluttered as apps pile up.

We build custom sections your team can rearrange, keep apps to the ones that earn their place, and set up SEO and tracking properly. Migrations include full redirect mapping so rankings carry over.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

## Questions about Shopify Development {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Shopify or WooCommerce?
Shopify suits teams who want managed hosting and simple operations. WooCommerce suits those who want full control. We recommend case by case.

? Can you migrate us from another platform?
Yes, including products, customers, orders where possible, and URL redirects.

? Do you support Indian payment gateways?
Yes. We configure gateways that support UPI, cards and wallets.
:::
