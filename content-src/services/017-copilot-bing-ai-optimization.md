## What Microsoft Copilot and Bing AI Optimization covers

> Many teams optimise only for Google and leave Bing half-configured. For B2B brands whose buyers live in Microsoft 365, that is a missed channel.

We set up Bing Webmaster Tools properly, review crawl and index reports, add IndexNow so new and updated pages reach Bing quickly, and check how Copilot answers your category questions today. Then we improve the pages and listings it should be using.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

## Questions about Microsoft Copilot and Bing AI Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is Bing still worth optimizing for?
Yes, especially for B2B. Copilot is built into Windows, Edge and Microsoft 365, and its web answers rely on Bing's index.

? What is IndexNow?
IndexNow is a protocol that lets your site notify participating search engines, including Bing, as soon as a page is added or updated.

? Will Bing work affect Google rankings?
Not directly, but the same improvements, such as clearer pages and fixed crawl errors, usually help both.
:::
