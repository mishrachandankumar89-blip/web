## What YouTube Advertising covers

> Video creates demand that search campaigns later capture, so measuring it only by direct clicks undervalues it.

We choose formats for each goal, build audiences from customer lists, interests and website visitors, guide creative so the message lands quickly, and track view-through and assisted conversions alongside branded search lift.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## Questions about YouTube Advertising {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Do we need professional video?
Not always. Clear, authentic videos shot on a good phone often perform well, especially for Shorts.

? How long should a YouTube ad be?
The message should land in the first five seconds. Total length depends on format and goal.

? How do you measure YouTube ad success?
Through conversions, view-through conversions, remarketing performance and changes in branded search.
:::
