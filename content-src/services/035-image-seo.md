## What Image SEO covers

> For products, recipes, designs and diagrams, image search can be a steady source of visitors.

We audit how your images are named, described, sized and loaded. Then we fix alt text and file names, convert heavy files to modern formats, add image sitemaps and markup, and make sure important images are not hidden behind lazy loading that crawlers cannot follow.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Image SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Does alt text help SEO?
Yes. Google uses alt text to understand images, and it is essential for accessibility.

? Should all images be lazy loaded?
No. The main image above the fold should load immediately. Images further down can be lazy loaded.

? Can images appear in Google Lens results?
Yes. Clear, original images on well-structured pages can be shown when people search visually.
:::
