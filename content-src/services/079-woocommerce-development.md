## What WooCommerce Development covers

> WooCommerce can do almost anything, which is also how stores become slow and fragile.

We build stores with a lean theme, a carefully chosen set of extensions, fast checkout and Indian essentials such as GST-ready invoices, UPI-friendly payment gateways and courier integrations. Caching and security are set up from day one.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.
