## What YouTube SEO covers

> Video results appear in Google search and are increasingly cited in AI answers for how-to questions.

We research the questions your audience asks on YouTube, plan videos to answer them, and optimise every upload: titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters and captions. We also review retention data to improve the next video.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about YouTube SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Do captions help YouTube SEO?
Accurate captions make videos accessible and give YouTube and Google more text to understand the content.

? How often should we publish?
Consistency matters more than frequency. One useful video a week or fortnight is a strong start.

? Can YouTube videos rank in Google?
Yes. Google often shows video results and key moments for how-to and review searches.
:::
