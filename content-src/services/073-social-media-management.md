## What Social Media Management covers

> An abandoned social profile looks like an abandoned business. Consistency builds trust.

We plan a calendar each month for your approval, create the posts, publish them, and handle community replies within agreed times. Monthly reports show what worked and what we will change.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.
