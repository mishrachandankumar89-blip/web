## What Keyword Research covers

> A keyword list without intent or priorities is just a spreadsheet. We turn it into decisions about which pages to build and improve.

We combine search data from tools such as Ahrefs or Semrush with your Search Console queries, sales conversations and People Also Ask questions. Every keyword group is matched to an existing or planned page, so pages do not compete with each other.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Search demand map.** Keywords grouped by topic, intent and buying stage.
- **Question research.** The questions buyers ask, ready for answer-first content.
- **Page mapping.** One primary page per keyword group, with gaps flagged.
- **Competitor gap list.** Valuable searches competitors win and you do not.
- **Priority plan.** Groups ranked by value, difficulty and current position.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## Questions about Keyword Research {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Do you provide search volumes?
Yes, from established tools, clearly labelled as estimates. We weigh them against intent and business value.

? How many keywords will we get?
As many as the market justifies. A focused local business may need 100 groups; a large store may need thousands.

? Does keyword research still matter with AI search?
Yes. It now includes question research, which feeds the answer-first pages that AI tools cite.
:::
