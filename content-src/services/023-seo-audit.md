## What SEO Audit covers

> Automated tools produce long lists of warnings. The value of an audit is deciding which ones actually matter for your revenue.

We combine a full crawl, Google Search Console data, real-user speed data and a review of your top competitors. Every finding comes with evidence, the reason it matters, the fix and an estimate of effort, ordered into a 90-day plan.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Technical review.** Crawling, indexing, speed, mobile, structured data and security.
- **Content review.** Coverage gaps, thin or duplicate pages and search intent mismatches.
- **Authority review.** Backlink profile, brand mentions and local citations.
- **Competitor comparison.** Where your top three competitors outperform you, and why.
- **Action plan.** Findings ranked by impact and effort with owners and dates.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Discovery.** Goals, margins and best customers, so we chase the right searches.
- **Audit.** Technical, content and authority review against your top competitors.
- **Roadmap.** A 90-day plan with the highest-impact fixes first.
- **Execution.** Fixes, rewrites, new pages and outreach shipped every week.
- **Review.** Monthly reporting and a quarterly reset of priorities.
:::
