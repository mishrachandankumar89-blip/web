## What ChatGPT SEO covers

> When ChatGPT searches the web, it relies on OAI-SearchBot and on what other trusted sites say about you. Both can be improved.

We start by checking that your robots.txt, CDN and firewall let OAI-SearchBot in, because a blocked crawler means no citations at all. Then we run your buyers' questions through ChatGPT, record which brands and pages it cites, and close the gaps with clearer pages, original evidence and mentions on the sites it already trusts.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

## Questions about ChatGPT SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Does blocking GPTBot stop ChatGPT from citing us?
OpenAI documents GPTBot as its training crawler and OAI-SearchBot as the crawler for ChatGPT search. Blocking GPTBot alone should not stop search citations, but blocking OAI-SearchBot will.

? Can you guarantee ChatGPT will mention us?
No one can. Answers vary by question and user. We measure your share of citations across a fixed question set every month so progress is visible.

? How quickly can things change?
ChatGPT search reads live pages, so improvements can appear within days or weeks of a page being crawled. Answers based on training data change more slowly.
:::
