## What Shopify SEO covers

> Shopify stores often look fine on the surface while loading a dozen app scripts and linking to duplicate product URLs.

We audit the theme and apps, fix internal links so they point to canonical product URLs, improve collection pages for category searches, and tune product templates. Then we connect Merchant Center so products can appear in free listings.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Shopify SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can Shopify URLs be changed?
The /products/ and /collections/ prefixes are fixed, but handles can be edited, with redirects created for old URLs.

? Do Shopify apps hurt SEO?
Some add heavy scripts that slow pages. We measure each one and remove or replace the costly ones.

? Is Shopify good for SEO?
Yes, with the right setup. Its hosting is fast and reliable, and most issues are fixable in the theme.
:::
