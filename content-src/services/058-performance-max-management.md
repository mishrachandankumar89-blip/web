## What Performance Max Management covers

> Performance Max uses one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps. That reach is powerful, and it needs supervision.

We set up value-based conversion tracking, build asset groups around real product or service themes, add audience signals from your customer data, and use the controls Google provides. Reporting separates brand from new demand so results are not flattered.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Track first.** No budget increase until conversions are measured accurately.
- **Structure for intent.** Brand, high-intent and research campaigns kept apart.
- **Write to the search.** Ads and landing pages that answer the exact query.
- **Prune weekly.** Search terms and placements reviewed, waste excluded.
- **Scale what works.** Budget moved to campaigns with proven returns.
:::

## Questions about Performance Max Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Should Performance Max replace Search campaigns?
Not always. Many accounts run both, with Search covering key terms precisely.

? Why does Performance Max spend on our brand name?
It can serve on branded searches. Brand exclusions and separate brand campaigns help keep reporting honest.

? Does it work for lead generation?
Yes, when lead quality is fed back through offline conversion imports or value rules.
:::
