## What Lead Nurturing covers

> Leads that go quiet are rarely lost for good. They are usually waiting for the right moment or the right answer.

We segment your leads, write sequences that answer each segment's questions and objections, agree hand-off rules with sales, and set up re-engagement for leads that have gone cold.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.

## Questions about Lead Nurturing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How long should a nurture sequence be?
Long enough to cover the main questions buyers have, often six to ten messages over several weeks.

? Should sales or marketing own nurturing?
Both. Marketing runs the sequences; sales defines when a lead is ready.

? Can nurturing work over WhatsApp?
Yes, for opted-in contacts, often alongside email.
:::
