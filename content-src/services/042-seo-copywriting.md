answer: SEO copywriting is writing web copy that matches what people search for and persuades them to act. ## What SEO Copywriting covers

> The best SEO copy is first of all good copy. Search engines reward pages that satisfy the reader.

We study the results that rank for your target search, interview your team about the offer, and write copy that answers the buyer's questions in the order they ask them. Each page gets a clear title, meta description and structure.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Intent brief.** What the searcher wants and what competing pages offer.
- **Page copy.** Headlines, sections, proof and calls to action.
- **Answer blocks.** Direct replies to the main question near the top.
- **Titles and descriptions.** Written to earn the click within length limits.
- **Two revision rounds.** Edits until it sounds like your brand.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Brief.** Search intent, questions to answer, sources and the expert to interview.
- **Interview.** A short call to capture real experience and examples.
- **Draft.** Answer first, evidence after, sources linked.
- **Edit and fact-check.** Every claim checked, every number dated.
- **Publish and refresh.** Performance reviewed after 90 days, then updated.
:::
