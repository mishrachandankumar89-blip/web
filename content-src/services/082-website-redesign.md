## What Website Redesign covers

> Many redesigns fail because they start with visuals and ignore what was already working.

We study analytics and search data to see which pages earn traffic and leads, keep what works, fix what does not, and design the new site with a performance budget. Redirects, tracking and content are verified before launch.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

## Questions about Website Redesign {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How often should a website be redesigned?
When it no longer serves your business, often every three to five years. Continuous improvement reduces the need for big redesigns.

? Can we keep our URLs?
Often yes, which is the safest option for SEO. Where URLs must change, redirects protect rankings.

? How long does a redesign take?
Typically eight to fourteen weeks for a business site, depending on scope and content.
:::
