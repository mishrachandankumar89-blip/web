## What Call Tracking covers

> Without call tracking, phone leads look like they came from nowhere, and good campaigns get cut.

We choose a provider that supports Indian numbers, set up dynamic numbers on your site, connect calls to Google Ads and GA4, and, with consent and disclosure, record calls for quality and lead scoring.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

## Questions about Call Tracking {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Will changing numbers confuse customers?
No. Calls forward to your existing line, and your main number stays in your Business Profile and citations.

? Can calls be recorded?
Yes, with disclosure and consent, in line with applicable law.

? Does Google Ads track calls already?
It tracks calls from ads and can track website calls with a forwarding number. A dedicated tool adds more detail.
:::
