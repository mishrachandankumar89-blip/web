## What WordPress Development covers

> Off-the-shelf themes and page builders are quick to start but often slow, bloated and hard to change later.

We build custom themes using WordPress's own block editor, so your team edits pages visually without a page builder. Custom content types, forms, integrations and structured data are built in, and every build passes speed and accessibility checks before launch.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.
