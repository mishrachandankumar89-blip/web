## What Perplexity SEO covers

> Perplexity searches the web for nearly every answer and names its sources, so the path from a better page to a citation is short and visible.

We confirm PerplexityBot is allowed, find the questions in your category where Perplexity cites forums, directories or competitors instead of you, and build pages that answer those questions more precisely. Fresh, dated information tends to matter here, so we also plan regular updates to key pages.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

## Questions about Perplexity SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Does Perplexity use my content for training?
Perplexity says PerplexityBot is used to surface and link websites in its search results and is not used to train AI foundation models.

? Why does Perplexity cite Reddit instead of us?
Forums often answer narrow questions directly. A page that answers the same question clearly, with evidence, gives Perplexity a better source to cite.

? Is Perplexity worth the effort for a small business?
Often yes, because its audience asks detailed research questions and the citations link straight to your page.
:::
