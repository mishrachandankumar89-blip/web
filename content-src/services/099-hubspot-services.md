## What HubSpot Services covers

> Many HubSpot portals use a fraction of what they pay for, with messy properties and broken workflows.

We audit your portal, clean up data and properties, rebuild workflows and pipelines around your real process, integrate your other tools, and train your team to use it day to day.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Portal audit.** Data, properties, workflows and integrations reviewed.
- **Pipeline setup.** Deal stages that match your sales process.
- **Workflows and scoring.** Automation and lead prioritisation.
- **Integrations.** Website forms, ads, calendars and other tools.
- **Dashboards and training.** Reports your team uses, and how to use them.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Audit.** Lists, consent records, deliverability and current journeys.
- **Map journeys.** Welcome, nurture, purchase and win-back flows.
- **Build.** Templates, automations, CRM fields and forms.
- **Send and test.** Subject lines, timing and content tested steadily.
- **Report.** Pipeline and revenue from each journey, monthly.
:::
