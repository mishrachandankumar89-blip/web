## What Headless and Next.js Development covers

> Headless builds can be extremely fast, or invisible to crawlers if rendering is handled poorly.

We build with frameworks such as Next.js and a headless CMS, using server-side or static rendering for content pages, clean routing, metadata and structured data, and editor previews so marketing teams are not slowed down.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

## Questions about Headless and Next.js Development {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is headless better for SEO?
Not by itself. It can be excellent or poor depending on rendering and implementation.

? Which headless CMS do you use?
We choose by project, including options such as Sanity, Contentful, Strapi or headless WordPress.

? Is headless more expensive?
Usually yes, to build and maintain. It pays off when the product truly needs it.
:::
