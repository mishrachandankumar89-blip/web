## Why social matters for search and AI too

> Most purchases start before the search. Social media is often where the idea first appears.

Short video has become the main way many people discover brands. The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your reels or posts is more likely to search your name, click your result over a stranger's and trust what they read.

Those brand searches feed back into everything else we do. Branded search volume, reviews, mentions and social profiles listed in your structured data all help search engines and AI assistants understand that your business is real, active and recognised.

## Organic and paid, planned together

### Strategy

Which platforms your buyers use, what they need to hear at each stage, and what success looks like. A B2B consultancy and a fashion label need very different plans, so we do not start from a template.

### Content

A monthly calendar built from content pillars: education, proof, behind the scenes and offers. We produce short video scripts, carousels, static posts and captions, and we work with your team to film real people and real work, which almost always outperforms stock footage.

### Paid social

Meta ads across Instagram and Facebook, LinkedIn ads for B2B, and YouTube for reach. We set up the Meta pixel and Conversions API, or the LinkedIn Insight Tag, so campaigns optimise toward real enquiries rather than cheap clicks.

### Community

Replies to comments and messages, saved reply templates for common questions and a clear process for handing leads to your sales team quickly, often through WhatsApp.

## Choosing the right platforms

| Platform | Best for | Typical formats |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | Consumer brands, lifestyle, local services, visual work | Reels, carousels, stories |
| Facebook | Wide reach, local communities, older audiences, lead forms | Video, lead ads, groups |
| LinkedIn | B2B services, recruitment, professional credibility | Document posts, thought leadership, lead gen forms |
| YouTube | Explainers, reviews, tutorials, long-term search traffic | Long videos, Shorts, in-stream ads |
| WhatsApp | Follow-up, support and repeat customers | Business profile, catalogues, broadcast lists |

We usually recommend doing two platforms well rather than five poorly.

## Questions about social media marketing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How often should we post?
Consistency matters more than volume. Three to five good posts a week on your main platform is a solid start for most businesses. We would rather post less and keep quality high.

? Do we need to appear on camera?
Not always, but it helps. Content with real people from your business tends to build trust faster. We keep shoots short and give you scripts so it does not take over your week.

? Can social media ads work for B2B?
Yes. LinkedIn is the strongest for targeting by job title, industry and company size. Meta can also work for B2B with the right offer and creative, often at a lower cost per lead.

? How do you measure success?
By leads, sales and cost per result, tracked through pixels, the Conversions API and GA4. Reach and engagement are useful signals, but we do not report them as the goal.
:::
