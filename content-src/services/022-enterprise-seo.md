## What Enterprise SEO covers

> On a big site, the biggest SEO wins and losses come from templates and releases, not individual pages.

We work with your product and engineering teams to build SEO into how the site ships: requirements in tickets, automated checks before release, monitoring after it, and dashboards that show leaders which sections are growing, which are stalling and why.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Template audit.** Titles, headings, links and markup reviewed at template level.
- **Crawl and index strategy.** Rules for parameters, facets, pagination and archives.
- **Release safeguards.** Pre-launch SEO checks and post-release monitoring.
- **Internal linking system.** Hubs and modules that spread authority to deep pages.
- **Governance playbook.** Who owns what, with standards every team can follow.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.

## Questions about Enterprise SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How many pages counts as enterprise?
There is no fixed line. Sites with tens of thousands of URLs, several stakeholders or frequent releases usually need an enterprise approach.

? Do you work inside our ticketing tools?
Yes. We write tickets in Jira, Linear or your tool of choice, with evidence, acceptance criteria and test steps.

? Can you monitor releases automatically?
Yes. We set up crawls and alerts that flag changes to titles, canonicals, noindex rules or status codes after each release.
:::
