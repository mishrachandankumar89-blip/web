## What WhatsApp Marketing covers

> For many businesses in India, WhatsApp is where enquiries actually turn into sales.

We set up your profile, catalogue and quick replies, connect click-to-WhatsApp ads from Meta, and track chats as leads. For larger teams, we help set up the Business Platform with templates, opt-ins and CRM integration.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

## Questions about WhatsApp Marketing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can we send bulk WhatsApp messages?
Only to people who have opted in, using approved templates through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

? Do we need the API?
Small businesses can start with the free WhatsApp Business app. Larger volumes and CRM integration need the Business Platform.

? Can WhatsApp chats be tracked in analytics?
Yes. We tag click-to-chat links and log conversations as leads.
:::
