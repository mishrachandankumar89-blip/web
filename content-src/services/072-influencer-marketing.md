## What Influencer Marketing covers

> Follower counts are easy to fake. Relevance and genuine engagement matter far more.

We find and vet creators, negotiate terms, write briefs, review content before it goes live, ensure paid partnerships are clearly disclosed in line with ASCI guidelines in India, and measure sales and leads through tracked links and codes.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

## Questions about Influencer Marketing {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Micro or big influencers?
Smaller creators often bring higher engagement and trust for a lower cost. The right mix depends on your goal.

? Do influencer posts need to be labelled as ads?
Yes. In India, ASCI guidelines require clear disclosure of paid or sponsored content.

? Can we reuse creator content in our ads?
Yes, if usage rights are agreed in the contract.
:::
