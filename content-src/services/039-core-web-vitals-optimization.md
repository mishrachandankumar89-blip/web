## What Core Web Vitals Optimization covers

> Google's good thresholds are LCP of 2.5 seconds or less, INP of 200 milliseconds or less and CLS of 0.1 or less, measured at the 75th percentile of visits.

We find which templates fail which metric on which device, trace the cause in the code, and fix it: image delivery, render-blocking resources, heavy scripts, third-party widgets, fonts and layout shifts. Then we watch field data until it passes.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

## Questions about Core Web Vitals Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Why is our lab score bad when field data passes?
Lab tests simulate a slow phone and network. Field data reflects real visitors and is what Google uses.

? Will passing Core Web Vitals boost rankings?
It removes a disadvantage, but content and relevance matter more. The bigger gain is usually conversion.

? How long until field data updates?
The Chrome UX Report uses a 28-day rolling window, so improvements show gradually over about a month.
:::
