## How Google decides local rankings

> Google is unusually open about local search. It names three factors: relevance, distance and prominence.

**Relevance** is how well your Business Profile matches the search. Complete categories, services, descriptions and attributes help Google connect you to the right queries.

**Distance** is how far you are from the searcher, or from the location named in the search. You cannot change where you are, but you can make sure Google knows exactly where that is and which areas you serve.

**Prominence** is how well known you are. Google says this draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating also factor into local ranking. This is where most of the work, and most of the opportunity, sits.

India is a particularly strong market for local search. The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, with rural India making up 57 percent of them. Many of these users search by voice and in Indian languages, which makes clear, consistent business information even more important.

## Questions about local SEO {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can I rank in an area where I do not have an office?
Distance is a ranking factor, so it is hard to rank in the map far from your address. Service-area businesses can define the areas they serve, and location pages on your website can still rank in normal results for those areas.

? How many reviews do we need?
There is no magic number. What helps is a steady flow of recent, genuine reviews and a rating close to or above your competitors. A burst of reviews followed by silence looks less natural than a few every month.

? Should we use a virtual office address?
Google's guidelines do not allow virtual offices that are not staffed during business hours. Using one risks suspension. If you work from home and visit customers, set up as a service-area business and hide the address.

? How long does local SEO take?
Profile fixes can lift visibility within weeks. Prominence, built through reviews, citations and links, grows over three to six months.
:::
