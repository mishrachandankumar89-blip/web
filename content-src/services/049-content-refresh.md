## What Content Refresh and Optimization covers

> Pages lose rankings as facts age and competitors publish better answers. Many sites have dozens of articles quietly decaying.

We use Search Console to find pages losing impressions or clicks, compare them with what now ranks, and update each one properly. Pages covering the same topic are merged so they stop competing with each other.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

## Questions about Content Refresh and Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Is changing the date enough?
No. Updating the date without real changes does not help and can mislead readers.

? How quickly do refreshed pages recover?
Often within weeks of being recrawled, especially pages that once ranked well.

? Should we delete old posts?
Only if they have no value. Most can be improved, merged or redirected.
:::
