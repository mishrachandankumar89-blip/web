## What Review Management covers

> Google says review count and rating factor into local ranking. Buyers read reviews before they call.

We set up a simple request flow inside your existing process, write reply templates your team can personalise, monitor reviews across Google and the platforms in your industry, and report on volume, rating and themes customers mention.

Google says local prominence draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in. Its spam policies name link schemes, including bought links that pass ranking credit, as a reason to demote a site. Earned authority is the only kind that lasts.

## Questions about Review Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Can we offer a discount for reviews?
No. Incentivised reviews break Google's policies and many platform rules.

? Should we reply to every review?
Yes. Replies show you listen, and they help future customers judge how you handle problems.

? Which platforms matter besides Google?
It depends on your industry, for example Practo for clinics or Zomato for restaurants.
:::
