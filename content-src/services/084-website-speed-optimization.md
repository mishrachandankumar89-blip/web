## What Website Speed Optimization covers

> A Google-commissioned Deloitte study linked a 0.1 second mobile speed improvement to an 8.4 percent lift in retail conversions.

We measure your key templates, trace what slows them down, and fix the causes in order of impact. Changes are tested so nothing breaks, and real-user data is tracked until it improves.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

## Questions about Website Speed Optimization {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? Will a speed plugin fix everything?
It helps with caching and minification, but slow hosting, large images and heavy scripts need direct fixes.

? Does speed affect SEO?
It is part of page experience, but its bigger impact is on conversion and bounce.

? How fast is fast enough?
Aim for Core Web Vitals in the good range for at least 75 percent of real visits.
:::
