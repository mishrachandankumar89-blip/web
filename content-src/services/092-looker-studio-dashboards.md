## What Looker Studio Dashboards covers

> Dashboards fail when they show every metric available instead of the ones that matter.

We agree the key questions with you, connect GA4, Search Console, ad platforms and your CRM, and build clean Looker Studio dashboards with consistent definitions, filters and short explanations.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Metric definitions.** Agreed KPIs and how each is calculated.
- **Data connections.** GA4, Search Console, ads and CRM sources.
- **Executive view.** One page with the numbers leaders need.
- **Channel views.** Detail for SEO, paid, social and email teams.
- **Training.** How to read, filter and share the dashboards.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.

## How we deliver it {wide}

^ The process

::: steps
- **Measure.** Fix tracking and set a reliable baseline.
- **Research.** Analytics, recordings, surveys and expert review.
- **Hypothesise.** What to change, why, and the result we expect.
- **Test.** A/B tests where traffic allows, careful comparison where it does not.
- **Roll out.** Ship winners, record learning, move to the next idea.
:::
