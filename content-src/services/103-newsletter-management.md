## What Newsletter Management covers

> Many newsletters stop after three issues because nobody owns them. We take ownership.

We plan an editorial calendar, draft each issue from your expertise and news, design it for mobile, send it on schedule and grow your list through consented sign-ups on your site and social channels.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.

## Questions about Newsletter Management {wide alt}

^ FAQ

::: faq
? How often should a newsletter go out?
Weekly, fortnightly or monthly all work if the schedule is consistent and each issue is useful.

? Can the newsletter reuse blog content?
Yes, with a short summary and fresh commentary rather than a plain list of links.

? Should we use LinkedIn newsletters too?
For B2B, a LinkedIn newsletter can complement email and grow quickly.
:::
