## What Website Maintenance and Care covers

> Most hacked WordPress sites are running outdated plugins. Regular, careful updates prevent most problems.

We test updates on a staging copy, apply them, keep daily backups off site, monitor uptime and security, check forms and key pages, and handle small edits your team requests within agreed times.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

## What you get {wide alt}

^ Deliverables

::: grid 3
- **Tested updates.** Core, theme and plugins updated safely.
- **Daily backups.** Off-site copies with tested restores.
- **Security monitoring.** Scans, firewall rules and login protection.
- **Uptime and form checks.** Alerts if the site or forms stop working.
- **Small changes.** Content edits and fixes each month.
- **Monthly report.** What changed, what it delivered and what we will do next, in plain language.
:::
