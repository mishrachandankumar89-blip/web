## What Marketplace Listing Optimization covers

> A listing written once and copied to every marketplace usually underperforms on all of them.

We research how shoppers search on each platform, rewrite titles and attributes to each marketplace's guidelines, improve images and size information, and fix variation and catalogue errors that hide products.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, and Baymard Institute puts average online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Reach is large, but so is the drop-off, which is why clean listings, accurate feeds and a smooth checkout matter as much as traffic.
