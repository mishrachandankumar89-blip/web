## What Marketing Automation covers

> Most leads are not ready to buy on their first visit. Automation keeps the conversation going without manual effort.

We map your customer journey, define triggers and lead scores, build email, SMS and WhatsApp flows with consent, connect them to your CRM, and review performance monthly.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.
