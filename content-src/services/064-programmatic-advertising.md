## What Programmatic Advertising covers

> Programmatic reach is huge, which is exactly why controls matter: without them, budgets can drift to low-quality inventory.

We plan campaigns around clear audiences and goals, apply brand safety, viewability and fraud filters, use curated inventory where it helps, and measure with lift studies or holdout tests rather than clicks alone.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

? What budget does programmatic need?

With verification partners, curated inventory and close monitoring of placements and traffic quality.
