For a clinic in Lucknow, a coaching centre in Kota or a restaurant in Kochi, the most valuable piece of online real estate is not the website. It is the small map with three businesses that appears when someone searches for a service nearby.

Local search in India has grown with the internet itself. The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, with 57 percent of them in rural India and 44 percent having used AI-enabled features such as voice search, image search or chatbots. Many of these searches are spoken, many are in Indian languages, and a growing share now get an AI-written answer before any list of results.

This guide covers what Google actually uses to rank local businesses and what to do about each factor.

## The three factors Google names

Google's Business Profile help centre says local results are based mainly on three things:

- **Relevance:** how well your profile matches what someone searched for.
- **Distance:** how far you are from the searcher or from the location in the search.
- **Prominence:** how well known your business is, both offline and online. Google says this draws on information such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in.

You cannot move your shop closer to every customer. You can make your profile more relevant and your business more prominent, and that is where local SEO does its work.

## Step 1: Build a complete Google Business Profile

A half-filled profile is the most common reason a good local business ranks below a weaker competitor. Go through every section:

- **Business name** exactly as it appears on your signboard and documents. Adding keywords or locations to the name breaks Google's guidelines and risks suspension.
- **Primary category**, the most specific one that describes your main service, plus relevant secondary categories.
- **Address or service area.** Show your address if customers visit you. If you visit them, set service areas and hide the address.
- **Phone number and website**, matching what is on your site.
- **Hours**, including special hours for festivals and holidays such as Diwali, Eid and Christmas.
- **Services and products** with short descriptions and prices where you are comfortable sharing them.
- **Attributes** such as wheelchair access, payment methods or women-owned business, where they apply.
- **Photos** of the outside, inside, team and work. Real photos build trust far better than stock images.
- **Description** in plain language: what you do, for whom and where.

Then keep it alive. Post updates, offers and events, add new photos, and answer the questions people ask on the profile.

## Step 2: Get your details identical everywhere

Search engines and AI assistants cross-check your business across the web. If Google shows one phone number, Justdial another and your website a third, you look less certain and less trustworthy.

List every place your business appears and make the name, address and phone number identical, down to abbreviations like "Rd" and "Road":

| Platform type | Examples in India |
|---|---|
| Maps and search | Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect |
| Local directories | Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART for B2B suppliers |
| Industry platforms | Practo for clinics, Zomato and Swiggy for restaurants, MagicBricks or 99acres for property |
| Social profiles | Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube |
| Your website | Contact page, footer and structured data |

## Step 3: Earn reviews the right way

Reviews influence both rankings and decisions. A practical system looks like this:

1. **Ask at the right moment**, when the customer is happiest: after a successful treatment, delivery or project milestone.
2. **Make it easy** with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp or SMS.
3. **Ask everyone**, not only customers you expect to be positive. Filtering who you ask, often called review gating, goes against Google's policies.
4. **Reply to every review**, thanking positive reviewers by name and responding calmly and specifically to criticism.
5. **Never buy reviews or offer rewards** for them. Google removes fake reviews and can restrict profiles that use them.

A steady trickle of recent reviews looks more natural and helps more than a sudden burst followed by silence.

## Step 4: Give your website local substance

Your website supports your profile. Make sure it has:

- A **contact page** with your full address, phone, map and hours, matching your profile exactly.
- **Service pages** that explain what you offer in plain language, with local details where they matter.
- **Location pages** for each branch or major service area, written individually with directions, landmarks, team members and area-specific information. Copying one page and swapping the city name helps nobody.
- **LocalBusiness structured data** with your name, address, phone, hours and coordinates.
- **Pages in Hindi or other Indian languages** if your customers search in them, with proper language tags.

## Step 5: Become known locally

Prominence grows when other people talk about you. Local newspapers and news sites, city blogs, community groups, trade associations, college events, charity work and local sponsorships all create mentions and links. They also give AI assistants independent evidence about who you are and what you are known for.

## Local search and AI answers

People increasingly ask AI assistants for local recommendations, such as "good physiotherapist near Salt Lake for back pain" or "which CA firms in Pune work with startups". Google's AI Mode launched in India in 2025 and now works in Hindi and several other Indian languages. The answers draw on Business Profile data, reviews, local articles and websites.

The same work that wins the map makes you a stronger candidate there:

- Clear statements on your website of what you do, where and for whom.
- Consistent facts across every platform.
- Reviews that mention specific services and areas.
- Mentions on local sites that AI tools can read.

## Frequently asked questions

::: faq
? Can I list my business at home on Google Maps?
Yes, if you meet customers there or serve customers at their location. If customers do not visit your home, set it up as a service-area business and hide the address.

? Does Justdial still matter for SEO?
It is one of many places where your details should be correct and consistent, and it still brings direct enquiries in many categories. Treat it as part of your citation set rather than a ranking shortcut.

? How long does it take to rank in the local pack?
Profile fixes can show results within a few weeks. Building prominence through reviews, citations and mentions usually takes three to six months in competitive areas.

? Should I create separate profiles for each service?
No. One location should have one profile. Add services within it. Separate profiles are only for genuinely separate locations or legally distinct businesses at the same address.
:::
