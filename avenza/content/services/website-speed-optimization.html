The short answer

How do you make a website faster?

Making a website faster starts with measuring real-user data and finding the biggest causes. Common fixes include better hosting and caching, a CDN, modern image formats sized correctly, removing or deferring heavy scripts and plugins, loading fonts efficiently and reserving space for images and embeds. Each change is verified in field data.

FAQ

Questions about Website Speed Optimization

Will a speed plugin fix everything?

It helps with caching and minification, but slow hosting, large images and heavy scripts need direct fixes.

Does speed affect SEO?

It is part of page experience, but its bigger impact is on conversion and bounce.

How fast is fast enough?

Aim for Core Web Vitals in the good range for at least 75 percent of real visits.
