The short answer

How do you optimize for voice search?

Voice search optimization focuses on the natural, spoken questions people ask assistants, then answers them in short, clear passages a device can read aloud. It also depends on accurate local business data, fast mobile pages and, in India, content that reflects how people actually speak, including Hindi and regional languages.

N° 01

What Voice Search Optimization covers

Spoken searches are longer and more conversational than typed ones, and the assistant usually gives a single answer.

We research spoken-style questions in your category, write concise answer passages, strengthen local data such as hours and directions, and test how assistants respond. The same work tends to improve featured snippets and AI answers too.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about Voice Search Optimization

Is voice search different from AEO?

It is a close cousin. Voice adds spoken phrasing, local intent and very short answers.

Do Indian users search by voice?

Yes. The IAMAI and Kantar 2025 report found that 44 percent of Indian internet users had used AI-enabled features such as voice search.

Can we track voice searches?

Not separately in most tools. We track the question queries and snippets that voice answers draw on.
