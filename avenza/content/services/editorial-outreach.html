The short answer

Is guest posting still safe for SEO?

Guest posting is safe when it means writing genuinely useful articles for respected publications in your field. What Google's spam policies target is large-scale guest posting with keyword-rich links, often on low-quality sites, done mainly for rankings. We pitch real expertise to relevant editors and accept whatever link policy they set.

What Editorial Outreach and Guest Articles covers

A single article on a site your buyers trust is worth more than dozens on sites nobody reads.

We build a list of relevant publications, study what each one publishes, and pitch article ideas drawn from your experts' knowledge. We write the drafts with your experts, handle edits with editors, and report the results.

Google says local prominence draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in. Its spam policies name link schemes, including bought links that pass ranking credit, as a reason to demote a site. Earned authority is the only kind that lasts.

FAQ

Questions about Editorial Outreach and Guest Articles

Do you buy guest posts?

No. We do not pay for links that pass ranking credit, as it breaks Google's spam policies.

What if the publication uses nofollow links?

That is fine. The value comes from authority, readers and brand mentions, not only link credit.

How many articles per month?

It depends on your expertise and the market. Quality and relevance come first.
