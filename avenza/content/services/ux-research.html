The short answer

Why use heatmaps and session recordings?

Heatmaps and session recordings show what visitors actually do on your pages: where they click, how far they scroll, where they hesitate and where they leave. Combined with surveys and interviews explaining why, they reveal friction that analytics numbers alone cannot, and give conversion improvements a solid evidence base.

N° 01

What UX Research and Heatmaps covers

Analytics tells you where people leave. Research tells you why.

We install privacy-conscious tools, review recordings and heatmaps for key pages, run short surveys and user interviews, and summarise the patterns into a clear list of problems and opportunities.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

The process

How we deliver it

  1. Measure Fix tracking and set a reliable baseline.
  2. Research Analytics, recordings, surveys and expert review.
  3. Hypothesise What to change, why, and the result we expect.
  4. Test A/B tests where traffic allows, careful comparison where it does not.
  5. Roll out Ship winners, record learning, move to the next idea.

FAQ

Questions about UX Research and Heatmaps

Are session recordings legal?

Yes, when disclosed in your privacy policy and configured to mask personal data.

Which tools do you use?

Tools such as Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar or similar, chosen for your needs.

How many recordings do you review?

Enough to see consistent patterns, usually several hundred sessions per key page.
