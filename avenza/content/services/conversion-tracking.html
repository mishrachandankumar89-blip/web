The short answer

What is conversion tracking?

Conversion tracking records valuable actions people take after interacting with your marketing, such as form submissions, calls, WhatsApp chats or purchases, and sends them to analytics and ad platforms. Accurate tracking lets bidding systems optimise toward real customers and shows which channels and campaigns deserve more budget.

N° 01

What Conversion Tracking covers

Duplicated or missing conversions are the most common reason ad accounts underperform.

We map every conversion that matters, implement it through Tag Manager and server-side connections where useful, add values, and import offline outcomes from your CRM so platforms learn which leads became customers.

Baymard Institute's running average across 50 studies puts online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Not all of it is fixable, but slow pages, surprise costs, long forms and missing proof are. Every improvement in conversion makes every other marketing channel cheaper per lead.

FAQ

Questions about Conversion Tracking

Why do platforms report different numbers?

Each uses its own attribution rules and windows. We align definitions and explain the differences.

Can you track WhatsApp leads?

Yes, through tagged click-to-chat links and logging chats as leads.

What are offline conversion imports?

Uploading CRM outcomes, such as closed deals, to ad platforms so they optimise for real sales.
