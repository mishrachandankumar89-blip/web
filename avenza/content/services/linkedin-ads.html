The short answer

When are LinkedIn ads worth the higher cost?

LinkedIn ads are worth their higher cost when the value of a customer is high and the buyer can be described by job title, function, seniority, industry or company. Account-based targeting, lead gen forms and useful content offers reach decision makers that other platforms cannot isolate as precisely.

What LinkedIn Ads covers

LinkedIn clicks cost more than most platforms, so the offer and targeting must be sharp.

We build audiences from target account lists, job functions and seniority, choose formats such as document ads and lead gen forms, and create offers buyers actually want. The Insight Tag and CRM feedback show which campaigns produce real pipeline.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.
