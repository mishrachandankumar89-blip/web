The short answer

What is product feed management?

Product feed management maintains the structured data files that send your product information to Google Merchant Center, Meta catalogues, marketplaces and comparison sites. It keeps prices and stock accurate, enriches attributes such as brand, colour and size, optimises titles for search, fixes disapprovals and adds custom labels for smarter advertising.

What Product Feed Management covers

A feed with missing attributes or disapproved products quietly limits your reach on every channel that uses it.

We audit your feeds, fix errors and disapprovals, enrich product data, optimise titles and categories, and set up rules or a feed tool so changes stay in sync with your store automatically.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, and Baymard Institute puts average online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Reach is large, but so is the drop-off, which is why clean listings, accurate feeds and a smooth checkout matter as much as traffic.

FAQ

Questions about Product Feed Management

Do we need a feed tool?

Small catalogues may not. Larger or multi-channel catalogues usually benefit from one.

Why are products disapproved?

Common causes include price or stock mismatches, missing identifiers and policy issues. We fix each one.

Does the feed affect free listings?

Yes. Free listings and Shopping ads both use your Merchant Center feed.
