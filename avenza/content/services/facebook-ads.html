The short answer

How do you run Facebook and Instagram ads well?

Running Meta ads well starts with reliable tracking through the Meta pixel and Conversions API, so the system optimises toward real customers. Then it is mostly creative: testing hooks, formats and offers steadily, while keeping campaign structures simple enough for Meta's delivery system to learn and feeding lead quality back.

What Facebook and Instagram Ads covers

On Meta, creative decides most of the results. Targeting matters less than it once did.

We set up the pixel and Conversions API, define the conversion events that matter, and run a steady creative testing rhythm with your team. Lead form campaigns are checked for quality, not just cost, and results are reported against sales.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

FAQ

Questions about Facebook and Instagram Ads

Do you create the ad creative?

We write scripts and copy, design statics and guide video shoots. We also edit footage your team captures.

Are lead form ads good quality?

They can be, with qualifying questions and quick follow-up. We track quality, not just volume.

What is the Conversions API?

A server-side connection that sends conversion events to Meta, making tracking more reliable.
