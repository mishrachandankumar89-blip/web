The short answer

How do D2C brands grow profitably?

D2C brands grow profitably by knowing their unit economics, especially customer acquisition cost against contribution margin and repeat purchase value. They test creative steadily on Meta and Google, convert visitors with a fast store and clear offers, and invest heavily in retention through email, WhatsApp and loyalty, because repeat customers make acquisition affordable.

What D2C Growth Marketing covers

Many D2C brands grow revenue while losing money on every new customer. Growth has to be planned around margin.

We start with your numbers: acquisition costs, margins and repeat rates. Then we plan acquisition, retention and conversion together, with weekly creative tests and monthly reviews of contribution margin by channel.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report counted 958 million active internet users, and Baymard Institute puts average online cart abandonment at about 70 percent. Reach is large, but so is the drop-off, which is why clean listings, accurate feeds and a smooth checkout matter as much as traffic.

FAQ

Questions about D2C Growth Marketing

Should D2C brands also sell on marketplaces?

Often yes, for reach, while keeping your own store for margin and customer data.

What is a healthy CAC?

It depends on your margin and repeat purchase value. We calculate it from your numbers.

How important is retention?

Very. Repeat customers usually cost far less to win back than new ones cost to acquire.
