Which AI crawlers should a business allow?

Most businesses that want to be recommended should allow search and answer crawlers such as Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot. Training crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot and CCBot are a separate decision. We set each rule deliberately and check that CDN and firewall settings match robots.txt.

FAQ

Questions about AI Crawler and llms.txt Setup

Is llms.txt a ranking factor?

No. It is a proposed standard. Google's May 2026 guide says it gets no special treatment in Search. It is cheap to add and may help some tools.

Does robots.txt protect private content?

No. It asks reputable crawlers to stay away but is not a security control. Private content belongs behind a login.

Will allowing AI crawlers slow our site?

Rarely. If a crawler strains your server, rate limiting at the server or CDN is more reliable than crawl-delay rules.
