The short answer

What is digital PR for SEO?

Digital PR is the practice of earning coverage, links and brand mentions on news sites, industry publications and trusted blogs by giving them something worth publishing, such as original data, expert commentary or a useful tool. It builds the authority that helps pages rank and gives AI assistants independent sources that confirm who you are.

N° 01

Why links and mentions still matter

On the web, what others say about you counts for more than what you say about yourself.

Links remain one of the clearest signals of trust between websites. Google's local ranking documentation lists links, articles and directories as part of prominence, and its wider systems use links to discover and weigh pages. For AI assistants, independent coverage plays a similar role: when several credible sites describe your brand the same way, a model has evidence to repeat.

The rules are also clear. Google's spam policies name link schemes, including buying or selling links that pass ranking credit, excessive link exchanges and large-scale guest posting with keyword-rich anchors, as violations that can demote a site. Since 2019 Google has asked sites to mark paid and sponsored links with rel="sponsored" and user-generated ones with rel="ugc".

So the only link building worth paying for is the kind that would be worth doing even if search engines did not exist: getting your expertise in front of people who care about it.

N° 02

What actually earns coverage

Original data

Journalists need numbers. A survey of your customers, an analysis of your own anonymised data, or a study that combines public datasets in a new way gives them a story with your name on it. One strong data story can earn coverage for years.

Expert commentary

Reporters often look for specialists to quote on breaking topics. We position your experts with clear bios and quick, quotable comments on the subjects they genuinely know, and respond to journalist requests through the platforms that collect them.

Useful tools and resources

Calculators, templates, checklists and clear explainers attract links from blogs, universities and industry sites because they save readers time.

Relationships and partnerships

Industry associations, events, suppliers, clients and local organisations often link to the businesses they work with. These links are relevant by nature and are frequently overlooked.

FAQ

Questions about digital PR

How many links will we get each month?

It varies with the story and the market, so we do not sell links by the dozen. We agree on campaign goals, such as coverage in specific publications or a number of relevant referring domains per quarter, and report against them honestly.

Are guest posts bad?

Not by nature. A thoughtful article for a respected publication in your field is fine. Mass guest posting on low-quality sites purely for links is what Google's policies target.

Do links without "dofollow" help?

Links marked nofollow, sponsored or ugc may not pass ranking credit in the same way, but coverage on a trusted site still brings readers, brand searches and mentions that AI assistants can see.

Can digital PR work for a B2B or niche business?

Yes, often better. Trade publications and specialist newsletters are hungry for data and expert views, and their readers are exactly the buyers you want.
