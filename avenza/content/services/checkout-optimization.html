The short answer

How do you reduce cart abandonment?

Reducing cart abandonment means removing friction at checkout: showing delivery costs and dates early, allowing guest checkout, keeping forms short with autofill, offering preferred payment methods such as UPI, cash on delivery and cards, displaying trust signals and return terms, making the page fast on mobile, and following up abandoned carts with reminders.

FAQ

Questions about Checkout Optimization

Should we force account creation?

Usually not. Guest checkout reduces abandonment; offer an account after purchase.

Does cash on delivery still matter in India?

For many categories, yes. Offering it alongside UPI and cards can lift completion.

Can you work on Shopify checkout?

Yes, within Shopify's checkout customisation options and apps.
