The short answer

What makes email marketing work in 2026?

Email marketing works when it is permission-based, relevant and technically sound. That means growing lists with clear consent, segmenting by interest and behaviour, sending useful content and offers at a sensible rhythm, authenticating domains with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, making unsubscribing easy, and measuring revenue or pipeline rather than opens alone.

What Email Marketing covers

Mailbox providers now filter hard on authentication and spam complaints, so deliverability is part of strategy.

We set up authentication, clean and segment your list, plan a campaign calendar, write and design emails that render well on phones, test subject lines and content, and report revenue by campaign.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.

FAQ

Questions about Email Marketing

Which email platform should we use?

It depends on size and needs, for example Mailchimp, Brevo, Klaviyo or HubSpot. We recommend after reviewing your setup.

Can we email a purchased list?

No. Purchased lists harm deliverability and are not permission-based.

How often should we email?

As often as you have something useful to say, often weekly or fortnightly.
