The short answer

How is B2B SEO different from B2C SEO?

B2B SEO targets smaller search volumes with higher value, longer sales cycles and several decision makers. It focuses on problem and solution searches, comparison and alternative pages, pricing and integration questions, and expert content, with success measured in qualified leads and pipeline rather than raw traffic.

N° 01

What B2B SEO covers

In B2B, a keyword with 50 monthly searches can be worth more than one with 50,000, if the right buyer is behind it.

We map searches to each member of the buying group, from the person with the problem to the person signing off the budget. Then we build pages that answer their questions honestly, including comparisons, and connect organic leads to your CRM.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about B2B SEO

Should we publish comparison pages?

Yes, if they are fair and accurate. Buyers search for them, and you can explain where you fit best.

How do you measure B2B SEO?

By qualified leads, meetings and pipeline influenced by organic search, alongside rankings.

How long does B2B SEO take?

Typically six to twelve months for meaningful pipeline impact, given longer sales cycles.
