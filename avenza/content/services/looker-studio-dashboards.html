The short answer

What should a marketing dashboard show?

A marketing dashboard should show the few numbers that drive decisions: leads or sales, cost per result, revenue by channel and trends over time, with drill-downs for the teams who need detail. It should update automatically, use consistent definitions and be simple enough that leaders read it without a walkthrough.
