The short answer

How do you run app install campaigns effectively?

Effective app campaigns optimise for valuable in-app actions, such as registrations or purchases, rather than cheap installs. That requires accurate measurement through Firebase or a mobile measurement partner, strong store listings, creative for each placement, and separate strategies for Google App campaigns, Meta and Apple Search Ads, reviewed against cohort retention.

What App Install Campaigns covers

Cheap installs that never open the app twice waste budget and distort your data.

We set up measurement first, improve your store listing, build campaigns that optimise for in-app events, and track cohorts so you can see which channels bring users who stay and spend.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

FAQ

Questions about App Install Campaigns

Should we optimise for installs or actions?

For actions, once enough data exists. Install-optimised campaigns can help in the very early phase.

What is an MMP?

A mobile measurement partner attributes installs and in-app events to the ads that drove them.

Does app store optimisation matter for ads?

Yes. A better store listing converts more of the people your ads bring.
