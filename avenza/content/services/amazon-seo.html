The short answer

What is Amazon SEO?

Amazon SEO is the practice of improving product listings so they rank higher in Amazon search and convert more shoppers. It covers keyword research inside Amazon, titles, bullet points, descriptions and backend search terms, high-quality images and A+ content, accurate attributes, competitive pricing and a steady flow of genuine reviews.

N° 01

What Amazon SEO covers

Amazon's search rewards listings that are relevant and that sell, so listing quality and conversion feed each other.

We research how shoppers search for your products on Amazon, rewrite titles, bullets and backend terms within Amazon's guidelines, improve images and A+ content, and fix catalogue errors that hide products. We coordinate with Amazon Ads so paid and organic work together.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about Amazon SEO

Do you work with Amazon.in sellers?

Yes, and with sellers on other Amazon marketplaces.

Can you get more reviews for us?

Only through policy-safe methods such as Amazon's own review request tools. We never buy or incentivise reviews.

Should we run Amazon Ads too?

Usually yes. Ads bring early sales that help organic rank, especially for new products.
