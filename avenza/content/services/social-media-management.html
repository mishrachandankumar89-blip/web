The short answer

What does social media management include?

Social media management covers planning a monthly content calendar, creating posts, designs and captions, scheduling and publishing across your chosen platforms, replying to comments and messages, monitoring mentions, and reporting on reach, engagement and leads. It keeps your brand active and responsive without your team handling it day to day.

We plan a calendar each month for your approval, create the posts, publish them, and handle community replies within agreed times. Monthly reports show what worked and what we will change.
