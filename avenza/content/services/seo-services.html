The short answer

What do SEO services include?

SEO services improve how often and how high your website appears in search results for the queries your buyers use. The work covers keyword and intent research, technical fixes so pages can be crawled and indexed, content that answers questions fully, internal linking, and earning links and mentions from trusted sites, measured against leads and revenue.

N° 01

What good SEO looks like in 2026

SEO is still the largest source of free, intent-rich traffic most businesses have. It is also the foundation every AI assistant builds on.

Google has said for years that most sites need patience with SEO. Its own guide on hiring an SEO notes that it usually takes four months to a year to put improvements in place and start seeing the benefit. That has not changed. What has changed is the standard of page that wins.

Since the March 2024 core update, Google has folded its helpful content system into core ranking and added spam policies against scaled content abuse, site reputation abuse and expired domain abuse. Google said the update cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent. Thin pages produced at volume, by people or by AI, now lose.

The pages that win share three things. They match the intent behind the search exactly. They show real experience and expertise. And they sit on a site that is technically clean, fast and easy to understand.

N° 02

Our SEO framework

We split SEO into four layers and work on all of them every month, starting with whatever brings the most value soonest.

Intent and keyword strategy

We map every important search to the stage of buying it represents: learning, comparing, choosing or buying. Then we map each search to one page on your site, so pages do not compete with each other. Volume matters, but intent matters more. A search with 90 monthly visits from ready buyers can be worth more than one with 9,000 curious readers.

Technical health

Crawling, indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile layout, structured data, redirects, duplicate content and internal links. Most sites carry years of small problems that quietly hold rankings back. Our Technical SEO service covers this layer in depth.

Content and on-page

Titles, headings, answers, depth and evidence on every page that matters. We rewrite pages that are close to page one and create pages for gaps your competitors already cover. Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created it, how, and why. We make sure each page answers those three questions clearly.

Authority

Links and mentions from relevant, trusted sites still separate pages of similar quality. We earn them through digital PR, partnerships and genuinely useful assets. We never buy links in bulk, because Google's spam policies treat link schemes as a reason to demote a site.

FAQ

Questions about SEO

How long does SEO take to work?

Google's own guidance says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Technical fixes on an established site can show results in weeks. New sites in competitive markets take longer.

Do you guarantee first position on Google?

No. Google's guidance warns against anyone who guarantees a number one ranking, and we agree. We commit to the work, the reporting and the honesty about what is and is not moving.

Is SEO still worth it with AI Overviews?

Yes. AI Overviews and AI Mode draw on pages that are indexed and rank well, and Google says the same SEO best practices apply. SEO is now the entry ticket to both classic results and AI answers.

Do you write the content or do we?

Either works. We can write everything, edit your drafts, or brief your team. For expert topics we interview your specialists so the content carries real experience.

Can you work with our developers?

Yes. We write clear tickets with the problem, the fix and how to test it, and we check the change once it is live.
