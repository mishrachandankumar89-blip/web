The short answer

What does a PPC audit check?

A PPC audit checks whether conversions are tracked correctly, how campaigns are structured, which search terms and placements spend money without results, whether bidding strategies suit the data available, how ads and landing pages match intent, and how budget is split. It ends with a prioritised list of changes and their likely impact.

What PPC Audit covers

Most accounts we audit lose money in a few predictable places: broken tracking, broad targeting without negatives, and ads sending traffic to generic pages.

With read-only access, we review your account in depth, quantify waste where we can, and explain each issue in plain language. You can implement the changes yourself or ask us to.

Google's Quality Score, rated from 1 to 10, reflects expected click-through rate, ad relevance and landing page experience compared with other advertisers on the same keyword. Accounts that score well usually pay less per click. Performance Max now runs one budget across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail and Maps, which rewards clean data and good creative.

The process

How we deliver it

  1. Track first No budget increase until conversions are measured accurately.
  2. Structure for intent Brand, high-intent and research campaigns kept apart.
  3. Write to the search Ads and landing pages that answer the exact query.
  4. Prune weekly Search terms and placements reviewed, waste excluded.
  5. Scale what works Budget moved to campaigns with proven returns.
