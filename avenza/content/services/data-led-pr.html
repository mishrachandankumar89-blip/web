The short answer

What is data-led PR?

Data-led PR creates original research, such as a survey, an analysis of your own anonymised data or a new look at public datasets, and turns the findings into stories for journalists. Because the numbers exist only on your site, publications and AI assistants that use them credit and link to you as the source.

N° 01

What Data-Led PR and Original Research covers

One strong data story can be cited for years, long after a normal press release is forgotten.

We find a question your market cares about, design the survey or analysis, check the method carefully, and publish a clear report with charts. Then we pitch the headline findings to relevant journalists and track the coverage it earns.

Google says local prominence draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in. Its spam policies name link schemes, including bought links that pass ranking credit, as a reason to demote a site. Earned authority is the only kind that lasts.

FAQ

Questions about Data-Led PR and Original Research

How big does a survey need to be?

It depends on the claim. We size samples so the findings hold up, and we publish the method.

Can we use our own customer data?

Yes, if it is anonymised and aggregated, and your privacy policy allows it.

How long does a campaign take?

Usually six to ten weeks from idea to first coverage.
