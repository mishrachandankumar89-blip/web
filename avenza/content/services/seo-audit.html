The short answer

What is included in an SEO audit?

An SEO audit reviews everything that affects how a website ranks: crawling and indexing, site speed and Core Web Vitals, structured data, content quality and coverage, internal links, backlinks, local signals and competitors. A useful audit ranks each finding by impact and effort, so you know exactly what to fix first.

N° 01

What SEO Audit covers

Automated tools produce long lists of warnings. The value of an audit is deciding which ones actually matter for your revenue.

We combine a full crawl, Google Search Console data, real-user speed data and a review of your top competitors. Every finding comes with evidence, the reason it matters, the fix and an estimate of effort, ordered into a 90-day plan.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about SEO Audit

How long does an audit take?

Most audits take two to four weeks, depending on site size and access to data.

Will you fix the issues too?

We can. Many clients continue with implementation, or we brief their developers with clear tickets.

How often should we audit?

A full audit yearly, and after any redesign, migration or large drop in traffic.
