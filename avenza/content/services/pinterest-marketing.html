The short answer

Which businesses should use Pinterest?

Pinterest suits businesses whose buyers plan visually before purchasing, such as home decor, fashion, beauty, food, weddings, travel, crafts and design. People search Pinterest for ideas, so pins work like search results with a long lifespan. Keyword-rich pins, organised boards and product catalogues can bring steady traffic for months.

What Pinterest Marketing covers

A good pin can bring visitors for months, unlike a social post that fades in a day.

We research Pinterest search terms, set up rich pins and your product catalogue, design fresh pins regularly, organise boards around topics, and use Pinterest ads for seasonal moments.

The IAMAI and Kantar Internet in India 2025 report found that around 588 million Indian internet users, 61 percent of the total, watched short-video content. A buyer who has seen your brand on social is more likely to search for you, choose your result and trust what they read.

FAQ

Questions about Pinterest Marketing

Is Pinterest popular in India?

It has a smaller but engaged audience, strongest in visual categories. We check your category's fit first.

How long do pins keep working?

Often for months, because people find pins through search.

Do we need new images for Pinterest?

Vertical images work best. We adapt your existing photos and design new pins.
