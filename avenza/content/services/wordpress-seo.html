The short answer

How do you improve SEO on a WordPress site?

Improving SEO on WordPress starts with configuration: an SEO plugin set up properly, thin archive and tag pages kept out of the index, clean permalinks and an XML sitemap. Then it covers theme and plugin speed, structured data, internal linking, and content built with native blocks rather than heavy page builders.

N° 01

What WordPress SEO covers

Most WordPress SEO problems come from defaults that were never reviewed and plugins that were never removed.

We review your theme, plugins and settings, set up Yoast or Rank Math properly, clean up archives, attachments and tags, and measure the speed cost of every plugin. We also train your team to publish posts that are well structured from the start.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about WordPress SEO

Which SEO plugin do you recommend?

Yoast SEO and Rank Math are both solid. The setup matters more than the choice.

Do page builders hurt SEO?

They can add heavy code that slows pages. Native blocks are usually faster and easier to maintain.

Can you work on our existing theme?

Yes. We improve what you have, and recommend a rebuild only when the theme is the real limit.
