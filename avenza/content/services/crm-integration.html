The short answer

Why integrate marketing tools with a CRM?

Integrating marketing tools with your CRM means every lead from forms, ads, calls and chats arrives automatically with its source, so sales can follow up quickly and nothing is lost. It also lets you send closed-deal data back to ad platforms and analytics, showing which campaigns produce revenue rather than just enquiries.

N° 01

What CRM Integration covers

Leads copied by hand from emails into spreadsheets get lost, delayed or misattributed.

We connect your website forms, ad lead forms, WhatsApp and call tracking to your CRM, map fields and sources, set up deduplication, and send outcomes back to Google Ads and Meta.

Since February 2024, Google has required bulk senders to authenticate email with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, offer one-click unsubscribe and keep spam complaint rates low. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its 2025 Rules make clear, recorded consent the basis for marketing messages.
