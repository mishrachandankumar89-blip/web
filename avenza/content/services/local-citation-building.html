The short answer

What are local citations?

Local citations are online mentions of your business name, address and phone number, usually on directories, maps and industry sites. Consistent citations help search engines and AI assistants confirm that your business is real and where it operates. Citation building means correcting existing listings and adding relevant new ones.

N° 01

What Local Citation Building covers

Inconsistent details, such as an old phone number on one directory, create doubt for search engines and customers alike.

We find your existing listings, correct mismatches, remove duplicates where possible, and add listings on the platforms that matter for your industry and city, from Bing Places and Apple Business Connect to Justdial, Sulekha or IndiaMART.

Google says local prominence draws on information from across the web, such as links, articles and directories, and that review count and rating factor in. Its spam policies name link schemes, including bought links that pass ranking credit, as a reason to demote a site. Earned authority is the only kind that lasts.

FAQ

Questions about Local Citation Building

How many citations do we need?

Quality and consistency matter more than count. The main maps, top local directories and industry sites come first.

Do citations still matter?

They support local prominence and help AI tools confirm your details.

Can you remove duplicate listings?

Often yes, though some platforms only allow the owner to request removal. We guide that process.
