The short answer

What is entity SEO?

Entity SEO is the work of helping search engines and AI systems understand exactly who your business is and how it relates to people, places and topics. It uses consistent names and facts across the web, Organization and Person structured data, sameAs links to official profiles, and content that clearly connects your brand to its expertise.

N° 01

What Entity SEO and Knowledge Graph covers

If Google is unsure whether two mentions refer to the same business, it cannot credit you fully. The same is true for AI models assembling an answer.

We build a single source of truth for your company facts, add structured data that ties your website to your official profiles, clean up conflicting listings, and make sure founders, experts and locations are described the same way everywhere they appear.

At Google I/O in May 2026, Google said AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion people a month and AI Mode has passed one billion monthly users. Pew Research Center found that when Google showed an AI summary, people clicked a traditional result on 8 percent of visits instead of 15 percent. Being named inside the answer now matters as much as ranking below it.

FAQ

Questions about Entity SEO and Knowledge Graph

Can you get us a Google knowledge panel?

No one can guarantee one. Panels appear when Google is confident about an entity, and consistent, well-sourced information makes that more likely.

What is sameAs?

A structured data property that lists your official profiles, such as LinkedIn or YouTube, so machines can connect them to your website.

Does this help AI search?

Yes. Consistent entity information gives AI assistants confidence to describe and recommend you accurately.
