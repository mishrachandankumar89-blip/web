The short answer

What makes a landing page convert?

A landing page converts when it matches the ad or link that brought the visitor, states the offer clearly in the headline, shows proof such as reviews or results, answers the main objections, keeps the form short, loads quickly on mobile and has one clear call to action. Testing then shows which version works best.

What Landing Page Design covers

Sending paid traffic to a general home page wastes much of the click.

We write and design pages for each campaign or audience, build them on your site or a landing page tool, connect forms to your CRM and tracking, and set up A/B tests when traffic allows.

A Google-commissioned Deloitte study found that a 0.1 second improvement in mobile site speed raised retail conversions by 8.4 percent and travel conversions by 10.1 percent. WCAG 2.2, published by the W3C in October 2023, sets the current accessibility standard, including minimum target sizes for buttons and links.

FAQ

Questions about Landing Page Design

Should landing pages be on our main site?

Usually yes, for simpler tracking and shared trust. Separate tools can work for fast testing.

Should landing pages be indexed?

Campaign-only pages are often noindexed to avoid thin duplicates. Pages meant to rank are built differently.

How many fields should a form have?

As few as you need to follow up well. Every extra field can reduce completions.
