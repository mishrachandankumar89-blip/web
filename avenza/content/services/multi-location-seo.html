The short answer

How do you do SEO for multiple locations?

SEO for multiple locations combines a strong brand website with a dedicated, useful page and a verified Google Business Profile for each branch. It adds consistent name, address and phone details everywhere, local reviews and links for each area, and reporting by location so weaker branches get attention first.

N° 01

What Multi-Location SEO covers

Copying one location page fifty times with the city name swapped rarely ranks, and it can look like doorway pages.

We design a location page template with real local substance, such as team, directions, services and local FAQs, manage Business Profiles in bulk, align citations, and run review programmes per branch. Reporting shows each location's visibility and leads.

Google's own guide to hiring an SEO says improvements usually take four months to a year to show their full benefit. Since its March 2024 core update, Google says it has cut low-quality, unoriginal content in results by about 45 percent, so the pages that win now are specific, useful and technically sound.

FAQ

Questions about Multi-Location SEO

Do franchise locations need separate websites?

Usually not. Strong location pages on the main brand site tend to perform better and are easier to manage.

How do you avoid duplicate location pages?

By adding genuinely local content for each branch rather than swapping city names.

Can head office keep control?

Yes. We set shared standards while allowing local updates such as photos and offers.
