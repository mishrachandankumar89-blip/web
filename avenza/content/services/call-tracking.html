The short answer

How does call tracking work?

Call tracking assigns different phone numbers to different marketing sources, often swapping numbers dynamically on your website based on how the visitor arrived. When someone calls, the system records the source, campaign and sometimes keyword, and can send the call as a conversion to Google Ads, GA4 and your CRM.

FAQ

Questions about Call Tracking

Will changing numbers confuse customers?

No. Calls forward to your existing line, and your main number stays in your Business Profile and citations.

Can calls be recorded?

Yes, with disclosure and consent, in line with applicable law.

Does Google Ads track calls already?

It tracks calls from ads and can track website calls with a forwarding number. A dedicated tool adds more detail.
