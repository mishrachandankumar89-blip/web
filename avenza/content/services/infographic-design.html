The short answer

Are infographics still useful for SEO?

Infographics are useful when they explain something genuinely complex or present original data clearly. They make pages easier to understand, give journalists and bloggers something to reference, and can appear in image search. To work for SEO and accessibility, each visual needs supporting text on the page and accurate alt text.

What Infographic and Visual Content covers

A clear diagram can do more than a thousand words, but only if the idea behind it is sound.

We start from your data or process, plan the story, design the visual in your brand style, and publish it with the explanation, alt text and sources on the page. Where relevant, we pitch it to sites that might reference it.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.
