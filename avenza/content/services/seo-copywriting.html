The short answer

What is SEO copywriting?

SEO copywriting is writing web copy that matches what people search for and persuades them to act. It combines search intent research, clear headings, a direct answer early on, specific benefits and proof, natural use of the language buyers use, and a clear next step, without stuffing keywords or padding the page.

N° 01

What SEO Copywriting covers

The best SEO copy is first of all good copy. Search engines reward pages that satisfy the reader.

We study the results that rank for your target search, interview your team about the offer, and write copy that answers the buyer's questions in the order they ask them. Each page gets a clear title, meta description and structure.

Google's guidance on helpful content asks who created a page, how and why, and its spam policies now target content produced at scale mainly to rank. Research presented at KDD 2024 found that adding statistics, quotations and citations made content noticeably more visible in AI-generated answers. Substance beats volume.

FAQ

Questions about SEO Copywriting

How long should a service page be?

As long as it takes to answer the buyer's questions well. Length follows substance, not a word count target.

Do you use AI to write copy?

We may use AI for research and outlines, but every page is written, edited and fact-checked by a person.

Can you match our brand voice?

Yes. We build a short voice guide from your existing material and calls.
