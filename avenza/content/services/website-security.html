The short answer

How do you keep a business website secure?

A business website stays secure through layered protection: reliable managed hosting, HTTPS everywhere, a web application firewall, strong login security with two-factor authentication, regular updates, least-privilege user roles, daily off-site backups and continuous monitoring, with a clear plan for responding quickly if something goes wrong.
