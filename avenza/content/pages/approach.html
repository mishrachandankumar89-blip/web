The short answer

How does AVENZA approach a new client?

AVENZA starts every engagement by mapping the questions your buyers ask and measuring how you appear for them in Google and in AI assistants. We then fix technical and data foundations, build answer-first content backed by evidence, earn outside mentions, and report monthly against enquiries, revenue and AI citations rather than vanity metrics.

N° 01

Why we work from a method

Marketing fails less from bad ideas than from good ideas done in the wrong order.

A beautiful content plan is wasted on a site Google cannot index. A link campaign is wasted on pages that do not answer the search. Ads are wasted when tracking counts the wrong conversions. The AVENZA Method exists to put the work in the right order, so each step makes the next one count.

It is also how we keep ourselves honest. Each stage has a defined output you can see and question.

N° 02

Stage I: Map the questions

We collect the real questions your buyers ask from Google Search Console, People Also Ask boxes, sales and support conversations, reviews, forums and AI assistants. Each question is tagged by buying stage, value to your business and the service or page that should answer it.

The output is a question map: a ranked list of the 50 to 200 questions that matter most, which becomes the backbone of content, SEO and AI visibility work.

N° 03

Stage II: Measure the gap

We run every priority question through Google and the major AI assistants, including ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot. We record where you rank, whether you are cited, which competitors are named instead, and whether the answers describe your business accurately.

Alongside this we audit your site's technical health, content depth and authority against your top competitors. The output is a visibility baseline that every later report is measured against.

N° 04

Stage III: Fix the foundation

Crawling and indexing problems, slow pages, broken tracking, inconsistent business details and missing structured data are fixed first. These are rarely exciting, but they set the ceiling for everything else.

We also create a single source of truth for your company facts: what you do, where, for whom, since when and with which credentials, kept consistent across your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories.

N° 05

Stage IV: Build what deserves to be cited

For each question in the map, we create or improve the page that should answer it. Each page opens with a direct answer, then gives the evidence: data, examples, expert insight, comparisons and sources. We write with your specialists so the content carries real experience.

Where the market lacks good information, we create it: original studies, calculators, pricing explainers and guides that other sites, journalists and AI tools want to reference.

N° 06

Stage V: Earn outside proof

Search engines and AI assistants look beyond your website. We earn mentions, reviews and links on the publications, directories and community sites your buyers and the AI tools already trust, through digital PR, partnerships and expert commentary.

N° 07

Stage VI: Report, learn, repeat

Every month we re-run the question set, update rankings and citations, and connect the results to enquiries and revenue in Google Analytics 4. Then we decide the next month's priorities together.

N° 08

How an engagement is structured

  • Weeks 1 to 2: discovery, access, question map and visibility baseline.
  • Weeks 3 to 4: audit findings and a 90-day roadmap with owners and dates.
  • Months 2 to 3: foundation fixes and the first round of priority pages.
  • Month 4 onward: monthly cycles of content, authority and optimisation, with a quarterly strategy review.

N° 09

Tools we use

We use the standard professional toolset, chosen for the job rather than for the logo: Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Looker Studio, Bing Webmaster Tools, PageSpeed Insights and the Chrome UX Report, crawlers such as Screaming Frog, research platforms such as Ahrefs and Semrush, Google Ads and Meta Ads Manager, and our own repeatable process for testing AI assistant answers.

FAQ

Questions about our approach

Do we have to follow every stage?

The order matters more than the scope. A small project might cover stages one to three in a few weeks. A full programme runs all six continuously.

How do you test what AI assistants say about us?

We run a fixed set of questions through each assistant on a regular schedule, record the answers and sources, and compare them over time. Answers vary, so we look at patterns across many questions rather than a single reply.

What do you need from our team?

Access to analytics and Search Console, a contact who can approve content, and 20 to 30 minutes with a subject expert for each major piece of content.

Can the method work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. You can keep content or ads in-house and use us for strategy, technical work and AI visibility. The method gives both teams a shared plan.
