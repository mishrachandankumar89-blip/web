The short answer

Who is AVENZA?

AVENZA is a digital marketing agency serving businesses in India and around the world. We help brands get found, trusted and chosen in Google search and in AI answers, through Generative Engine Optimization, Answer Engine Optimization, SEO, content, digital PR, paid media, social media, websites and conversion work.

N° 01

Why AVENZA exists

For twenty years, online visibility meant one thing: rank on page one. That definition is no longer enough.

Today a buyer might ask ChatGPT to shortlist three accountants, check what Google's AI Overview says about a treatment, watch two short videos on Instagram and only then visit a website. Each of those moments is a chance to be named, or to be skipped.

Most marketing is still organised in silos. One team chases rankings, another runs ads, a third posts on social, and nobody asks what an AI assistant says about the brand. The result is a lot of activity and very little clarity.

We started AVENZA to join those pieces into one strategy, built around a simple question: when your buyer asks, are you the answer?

N° 06

What we will never do

These are the lines we do not cross, whatever the budget.

  • Promise a number one ranking or a guaranteed AI citation.
  • Buy links in bulk, run private blog networks or hide paid placements.
  • Write, buy or filter reviews in ways that break platform rules.
  • Publish mass-produced pages that exist only to catch searches.
  • Hold your accounts or data hostage if we part ways.

FAQ

Questions about AVENZA

Is AVENZA a full-service agency?

We cover the channels that decide whether a business is found and chosen online: AI visibility, SEO, content, digital PR, Google Ads, social media, websites and conversion. We do not offer offline media buying or event management.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes, when there is a clear goal and a realistic budget for it. For smaller businesses we often start with one focused service, such as local SEO or a website rebuild, and expand once it pays for itself.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients in other countries and plan around their time zone for calls.

Can we hire you for a one-time project?

Yes. Audits, website builds, migrations and GEO baselines are often one-time projects. Ongoing services such as SEO and paid media work best on a monthly basis.
