Privacy Policy

Last updated on 7 October 2026. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices and rights you have.

The short answer

How does AVENZA use my personal data?

AVENZA uses the details you share through this website, such as your name, email, phone number and message, only to reply to your enquiry and provide services you ask for. We do not sell personal data. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by contacting us.

Who we are

This website is operated by AVENZA, a digital marketing agency. In this policy, "AVENZA", "we", "us" and "our" refer to AVENZA, and "you" refers to anyone who visits this website or contacts us through it.

For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, AVENZA is the Data Fiduciary for personal data collected through this website. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp at +91 96809 51099, or through the form on our contact page.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you fill in our enquiry form, call us or message us, we collect the details you choose to share. That usually means your name, email address, phone number, company website, the service you are interested in and your message.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our server records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the time of your visit. If analytics is enabled on this site, Google Analytics 4 collects information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type, using cookies or similar technologies.

Information we do not collect. We do not ask for sensitive information such as financial account details, health information or government identity numbers through this website. Please do not send such information through the form.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call
  • To prepare proposals and provide services you ask for
  • To keep a record of our communication with you
  • To understand how visitors use the website so we can improve it
  • To protect the website against spam, abuse and security threats
  • To meet our legal obligations

We use your contact details for marketing only if you have agreed to it, and you can opt out at any time.

Consent and your choices

When you submit the enquiry form, you are asked to agree that we may use your details to respond to you. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it, but it may mean we cannot continue a conversation or a service.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and, if you become a client, for the duration of our work together and any period required by law, such as tax and accounting rules. Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted or anonymised within 24 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and our business, such as our web hosting company, email provider and, where enabled, Google Analytics, and only as far as they need to provide their service. Some of these providers may store data outside India. Where that happens, we rely on providers with appropriate security commitments.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our clients or the public.

How we protect it

The website uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Form submissions are stored in a restricted area of the website that only authorised people can access. We keep software up to date and limit access to personal data to those who need it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update your data
  • Ask us to erase your data when it is no longer needed
  • Withdraw consent you have given
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it is not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, contact us by phone or WhatsApp at +91 96809 51099 or through the contact page. We will respond within the time the law requires.

Cookies

This website uses a small number of cookies needed for it to work, such as cookies that remember a logged-in administrator. If Google Analytics is enabled, it sets cookies to measure visits. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings; the site will still work, although analytics will not count your visit.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as research sources and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.
